# GTM Budget & Strategy: LivenessLink

## 📊 GTM Strategy Overview

You cannot sell a £144,000 Cyber Security platform to a Corporate Treasurer via Facebook Ads or cold calls from 22-year-old SDRs in a noisy call center.

The strategy is **100% High-End Account-Based Marketing (ABM) and Executive Dinners**. We must manufacture absolute trust and exclusivity.

## 🎯 Channel Strategies

### Channel 1: The "Chatham House" Dinner

CFOs are currently terrified of deepfakes but don't want to admit their vulnerabilities publicly.
| Action | Execution | Expected ROI |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Target Target | Hire a retired, well-known CFO to act as the "Host". | Instant credibility. |
| The Setting | A private room in Mayfair (London) or Tribeca (NYC). High-end steak, good wine. £500/head. | 12 CFOs attend. |
| The Pitch | Zero sales presentation. The Host just moderates a discussion: _"How is everyone handling the Hong Kong deepfake threat?"_ Let them scare each other. By dessert, hand out our metal business cards. | 1 Dinner = 4 Qualified £100k+ Pipeline Deals. |

### Channel 2: ERP Systems Integrators

| Target                     | The Pitch                                                                                                   | Incentive                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deloitte / PwC Cyber Teams | When they install a new SAP instance for a client, they recommend LivenessLink as the final security layer. | 20% trailing revenue share. Makes them look like bleeding-edge savants to their clients. |

### Channel 3: Analyst Relations (Gartner/Forrester)

Enterprise C-Suites buy what Gartner tells them to buy.
| Action | Execution |
|--------|-----------|
| Briefings | Schedule 60-minute briefings with the lead analysts covering "Identity and Access Management" (IAM). Explain why standard SSO has failed the age of AI. |
| Report Inclusion | Fight to get included in the upcoming "Cool Vendors in Cybersecurity" report. |
