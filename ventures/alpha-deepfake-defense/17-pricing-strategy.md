# Pricing Strategy: LivenessLink

## 💰 Pricing Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Value-Based Insurance**: We are pricing an insurance policy against catastrophic financial ruin. £100,000/year is extremely cheap if it secures £2 Billion in annual wire transfers.
2. **Never Charge by Seat**: We don't want to disincentivize companies from adding more people to the approval quorum. We charge based on the _Volume of Funds Secured_.
3. **No Self-Serve**: This is a high-touch, white-glove enterprise security product. Pricing is entirely hidden behind "Book a Demo".

## 💵 Unit Economics by Tier

### Gross Margin Reality

Biometric hardware signing is computationally cheap. Our true costs are **Insurance** and **Support**. We must maintain massive Cyber Liability policies, and we must provide 24/7/365 phone support to CFOs who get locked out of their phones while trying to close an M&A deal.

| Tier       | Estimated ARPU | Gross Margin                                |
| ---------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Corporate  | £48,000 / yr   | 85%                                         |
| Enterprise | £144,000 / yr  | 75% (Due to 24/7 white-glove phone support) |
