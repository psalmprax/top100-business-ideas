# Product Roadmap: LivenessLink

## Phase 1: "The Cryptographic Handshake" (Week 1-4) [MVP]
- **Core Feature**: Mobile App for Treasurers.
- **Biometric Signature**: Uses iOS/Android Secure Enclave to sign a voice snippet or video frame.
- **Verification Portal**: A dashboard for the employee to verify the "Signature" of the Boss's call.

## Phase 2: "Real-Time Watermarking" (Month 2-4)
- **Video Overlay**: Real-time cryptographic watermark injected into Zoom/Teams calls.
- **Voice Liveness**: AI-detection of synthetic artifacts (frequency analysis of voice cloning glitches).
- **ERP Integration**: Direct integration with Kyriba or SAP Treasury.

## Phase 3: "Treasury Immune System" (Month 5-6)
- **Rule-Based Escrow**: Payments over $100k are automatically locked until a 3-factor Liveness Check (Biometric + Voice + Hardware Key) is completed.
- **Cold Storage Approval**: Offline biometric verification for ultra-high transfers ($1M+).
