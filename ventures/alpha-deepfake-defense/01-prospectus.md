# Venture Prospectus: LivenessLink (Deepfake Treasury Defense)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The $25 Million Video Call"
Corporate treasury departments are being decimated by "Vishing" (Voice Phishing) and video deepfakes. 
- **The Case**: A treasury employee authorized a $25M transfer after a video call with a deepfaked CFO and fellow staff.
- **The Gap**: Standard MFA (SMS/Email) is useless if the employee *believes* they have verbal/visual authorization from their boss. 
- **The Stats**: $1.56B lost to deepfake fraud in 2025.

## 🟢 The Solution: LivenessLink
A "Zero-Trust" communication layer for high-value approvals. It uses **Hardware-Backed Liveness Verification**.
- **Key Innovation**: "Proof-of-Presence" watermarking. Every video/voice call for a transfer requires a real-time biometric "handshake" that is cryptographically signed and immutable. It's not just "detection"; it's **Authentication**.

- **Enterprise SaaS**: $5,000/mo for a dedicated treasury authentication server (Private Instance).
