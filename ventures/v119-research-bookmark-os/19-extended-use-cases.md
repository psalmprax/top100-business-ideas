# Extended Use Cases: SmartMark (Research Bookmark OS)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)

### Use Case 1: The "Why I Bookmarked This" Memo

Forces a 10-second voice or text memo when saving a link so you have context weeks later.

### Use Case 2: Page-Change Monitoring

Alerts you if a bookmarked research paper or article has been updated or retracted since you last viewed it.

### Use Case 3: Automated Research Threading

Links bookmarks that discuss the same topics (e.g., "Market Trends" or "Technical Spec") into a cohesive research timeline.

---

## Extended Use Cases (4-10)

### Use Case 4: Research "Triage" Wizard (Self-Service)

**Scenario**: User has 100+ unread bookmarks from a conference.
**Solution**: "Speed-Reader" UI that displays AI summaries of each link and asks the user to "File," "Archive," or "Read Now" in 5-second intervals.

### Use Case 5: Bibliography Sync (Integration)

**Scenario**: Exporting bookmarks to Zotero, Mendeley, or Notion.
**Solution**: Semantic mapping of web data to "Academic/Formal Citation" formats, allowing for one-click export to major research managers.

### Use Case 6: Knowledge Gap Analytics

**Scenario**: Identifying what the user _doesn't_ know about a research topic.
**Solution**: AI analysis of the current bookmark pool that suggests "Missing Perspectives" or related papers to ensure a balanced research project.

### Use Case 7: Research Continuity & High-Availability (SLA)

**Scenario**: A corporate R&D lab or private equity firm loses access to their collective knowledge base during a critical due-diligence period.
**Solution**: Enterprise-tier SmartMark provides 99.9% data availability with a 30-minute "Knowledge Restoration" SLA. Includes automated offline-first desktop synchronization and multi-region cloud backups to ensure research is never unreachable.

### Use Case 8: Mobile "Flash-Capture" Research (Mobile)

**Scenario**: A researcher finds a critical paper or market trend while reading on their phone during a commute.
**Solution**: Native, biometric-locked mobile share-extension. It performs on-the-fly AI summarization of the link and allows the user to record a quick voice-memo "Importance Score" which is filed and indexed in the main research board instantly.

### Use Case 9: Zero-Trust Research Isolation (Security)

**Scenario**: A firm is conducting high-security proprietary research and needs to ensure that even SmartMark administrators cannot see the saved links or notes.
**Solution**: Password-protected "Zero-Knowledge" Silos within the OS. SmartMark uses client-side encryption (AES-256) where the keys never leave the firm's infrastructure, ensuring absolute confidentiality for proprietary market intelligence.

### Use Case 10: Academic Integrity & Validity Audit (Compliance)

**Scenario**: A legal or academic institution needs to verify that no research in their shared library has been recently flagged for retraction, AI fraud, or debunking.
**Solution**: SmartMark maintains an "Integrity Audit" log that cross-references the entire research library with global retraction databases. It provides an immutable report of the "Validity Score" for all saved sources to satisfy compliance and peer-review audits.
