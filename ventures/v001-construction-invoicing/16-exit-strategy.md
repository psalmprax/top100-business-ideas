### Phase 1: Prove the "Two-Sided" Market (Years 1-2)

- [ ] Onboard 50 General Contractors paying the £350/mo SaaS fee.
- [ ] Achieve a 30% adoption rate among their Subcontractors clicking the "Early Pay" button.
- **Target**: Prove to acquirers that GCs _want_ the AI parsing, and Subs _want_ the liquidity.

### Phase 2: Grow the Loan Book (Years 3-4)

- [ ] Secure a massive £50M+ debt warehouse facility from a tier-1 bank (e.g., Goldman, SVB).
- [ ] Process £500M+ in Annual Payment Volume.
- **Target**: Make our revenue base overwhelmingly derived from financial physics (the 2% rake), making us an incredibly lucrative acquisition for Procore to ingest.
