# GTM Budget & Strategy: Structura (Construction Invoicing)

## 📊 GTM Strategy Overview

Construction is a highly traditional, relationship-based industry. Digital ad spend yields very low returns because project managers and GCs are on job sites, not browsing LinkedIn. 

Our Go-To-Market is **100% focused on direct outbound, channel partnerships (Procore), and in-person industry events.**

### Channel Mix (Year 1)
| Channel | % of Budget | Monthly Spend | Core Activity |
|---------|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| Trade Shows & Events | 40% | £8,000 | Sponsoring regional builder associations and booths at major expos. |
| Outbound BDRs | 30% | £6,000 | Pure cold-calling. Dialing GC main lines to ask for the "Accounts Payable Controller". |
| App Store Ecosystem | 20% | £4,000 | Optimization, reviews, and co-marketing within the Procore App Marketplace. |
| Content (Case Studies) | 10% | £2,000 | Filming video testimonials of Subs who saved their business using Early Pay. |

## 🎯 Channel Strategies

### Channel 1: The "Procore Draft"
Procore is the undeniable operating system of construction. We must draft off their momentum.
| Action | Execution | Expected ROI |
|----------|----------|--------|
| The Integration | Build a flawless, 1-click Procore integration. Offer 3 months free for Procore users. | Essential for GC adoption. |
| App Store Ranking| Aggressively incentivize our first 20 pilot users to leave 5-star text reviews on the Procore App Store. | High organic inbound lead flow. |

### Channel 2: Regional Trade Shows
Construction is highly localized. Builders in Texas don't talk to builders in London. 
| Strategy | Execution |
|----------|----------|
| Skip the giant national shows initially. | They are too crowded with software giants like Autodesk. |
| Target Regional Associations. | E.g., The "Texas Association of Builders". Sponsor the breakfast buffet for £3,000. Give a 10-minute speech on "Stop typing PDFs manually". Capture 50 business cards. |

### Channel 3: The Subcontractor Virality Loop (Product-Led Growth)
This is the holy grail of our GTM and costs £0 in marketing budget.
| The Trigger | The Action | The Result |
|-------------|------------|------------|
| We sell a GC. | The GC tells their 50 Subcontractors: "You must use this link to submit invoices from now on." | 50 Subs create an account on Structura. |
| The Upsell | Inside the Portal, the Sub sees the "Get Paid in 48 Hours" button. | We acquire the FinTech user for absolutely £0 CAC. |
