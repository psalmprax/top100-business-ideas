# Hiring Roadmap: Structura (Construction Invoicing)

## 🎯 Hiring Philosophy

### Core Values
1. **Mud on the Boots** - Empathy for the construction industry is mandatory. We don't hire people who sneer at blue-collar workflows.
2. **FinTech Rigor** - When writing code that moves money, "move fast and break things" is unacceptable. We require test-driven perfection.
3. **AI Pragmatism** - We use Vision-Language Models (VLMs) as a tool to solve an awful data entry problem, not as a cool gimmick.

### First 5 Hires
| Priority | Role | Impact | Timing |
|----------|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Senior Backend (FinTech) | Architecting the Stripe/Plaid ledger and double-entry accounting system. | Month 1 |
| 2 | AI / ML Engineer | Fine-tuning VLMs to flawlessly extract line items from crumpled, coffee-stained contractor PDFs. | Month 1 |
| 3 | Founding AE (Sales) | A salesperson who formerly sold Procore or worked as a Project Manager in construction. | Month 3 |
| 4 | Frontend (React Native) | Building the mobile app so foremen can approve invoices from the muddy job site. | Month 4 |
| 5 | FinOps / Risk Analyst | Monitoring the "Early Pay" loan book to ensure we aren't factoring fraudulent invoices. | Month 6 |

## 👥 Role Specifications

### Role: Senior Backend (Finagle/Ledger)
**Department**: Engineering

**Responsibilities**:
- Lead the integration with our BaaS (Banking-as-a-Service) provider.
- Build the core immutable ledger that tracks invoice status (Submitted, Approved, Lien Waived, Funded, Settled).
- Ensure strict PCI and SOC2 compliance on data handling.

**Requirements**:
- 5+ years building payment systems (e.g., at Stripe, Square, Adyen, or a Neobank).
- Deep understanding of double-entry accounting databases.
