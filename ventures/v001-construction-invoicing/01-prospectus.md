# Venture Prospectus: Constructiv (AI Progress Billing)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The 90-Day Wait"
Construction subcontractors face crushing cash-flow gaps. While a project is active, they wait 60-90 days for payment. 
- **The Bottleneck**: Minor clerical errors in the complex **AIA G702 (Application for Payment)** and **G703 (Continuation Sheet)** documents lead to GCs rejecting the entire monthly invoice.
- **The Gap**: Legacy ERPs like Sage or Procore are "too big" and clunky for the 1,000s of specialized trade subcontractors (HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing).

## 🟢 The Solution: Constructiv
A "Mobile-First" progressive billing app for subcontractors. It uses AI to ingest field reports and automatically generate perfectly formatted, G702/G703 compliant docs with **Synced Lien Waivers**.
- **Key Innovation**: "State-Specific Lien Matching"—the system knows the legal requirements for lien waivers in all 50 states, ensuring the GC has zero excuses for non-payment.

## 💰 Revenue Model
- **Subscription**: $199/mo per company (Unlimited invoices).
- **Financing Wedge**: Partner with supply-chain lenders to offer "Invoice Advance" based on AI-verified invoicing accuracy.

## 🌍 Market Potential
- **TAM**: $15B+ (US Specialty Trade Contractors market).
- **Urgency**: Prompt Payment Acts are making error-free invoicing a legal priority.
