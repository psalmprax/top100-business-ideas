# Outreach Strategy: Constructiv (Progress Billing Capture)

## 🎯 Target Personas

- **Construction Owner/Founder**: Tired of chasing General Contractors for money.
- **Office Manager / Accounts Receivable**: Spent 20 hours a month fighting with Excel G702s.
- **Project Manager**: Wants to ensure they aren't over-billing or under-billing.

## 🏢 Target Companies (Pilot Phase)

- **HVAC & Electrical Trade Subs**: High complexity of materials + high payroll costs.
- **American Subcontractors Association (ASA)** local chapters.
- **Construction Insurance Brokers**: They want to lower the risk of payment disputes.

## 💬 Outreach Scripts

### The "Lien Waiver Hell" Approach

> "Hi [Name], I've been talking to HVAC subs on r/Construction and the #1 complaint is 'Lien Waiver Hell'—waiting on GCs who reject invoices for minor G702 errors. We've built Constructiv, an AI tool that makes your progress billing error-proof and attaches the correct state-law lien waiver automatically. Can I send you a free 'G702 Audit' of your last 3 invoices?"

### The "Prompt Payment" Approach

> "Hi [Name], with the new state Prompt Payment Acts, GCs are legally required to pay you faster if your billing is perfect. We've automated the 'Perfect Billing' process for trade subs. Are you still doing your G702s in Excel?"

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy

- **r/Construction**: Focus on the "Subcontractor vs. GC" payment dynamic.
- **Instagram/TikTok/Reels**: "A Day in the Life of a GC Rejecting an Invoice" (Relatability content).
