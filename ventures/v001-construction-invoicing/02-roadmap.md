# Product Roadmap: Constructiv

## Phase 1: "The Perfect G702" (Week 1-4) [MVP]
- **Core Feature**: Mobile-upload of "Schedule of Values" (SOV).
- **Automation**: One-click generation of G702/G703 PDF forms.
- **Integration**: Basic QuickBooks sync for payroll matching.

## Phase 2: "Lien Master" (Month 2-3)
- **Legal Engine**: Automated Lien Waiver generation based on payment amount.
- **Digital Signatures**: Built-in legally binding e-signing for subcontractors and their suppliers.
- **GC Portal**: A "Review" link for General Contractors to approve billing in real-time.

## Phase 3: "Field-to-Finance" (Month 4-6)
- **AI Vision**: Upload photos of work-on-site; AI estimates "Percent Complete" for the line item.
- **ERP Bridges**: Formal integrations with Procore and Autodesk Construction Cloud.
- **Sub-tier Management**: Manage payments and lien waivers for your *own* suppliers.
