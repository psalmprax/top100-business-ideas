# Competitive Override Use Cases: Constructiv (Progress Billing)

Existing construction software giants like Procore, Sage, and Autodesk Construction Cloud are built for General Contractors (GCs) and billion-dollar developers. They treat Subcontractors as an afterthought. **Constructiv** is built specifically to defend the Subcontractor's cash flow against the GC.

Here are the specific use cases where Constructiv surpasses the competition:

## Use Case 1: Automated State-Specific Lien Waiver Matching
**The Competitor Way**: A user generates an invoice in QuickBooks or a generic PDF, then hopes they manually attach the correct legal wording for a "Conditional Waiver and Release on Progress Payment" for their specific state (e.g., California vs. New York).
**The Sentinel Override**: The "Lien Master" Engine. Constructiv knows the legal jurisdiction of the project. If you invoice for $54,000, it automatically generates a perfectly formatted, legally binding $54,000 progress lien waiver for *that specific state* and attaches it to the AIA G702 form. 

## Use Case 2: Field-to-Desk "Percent Complete" Defense
**The Competitor Way**: A GC disputes that the drywall is 50% complete, throwing out the invoice and delaying payment by 30 days while the Sub's Foreman tries to find emails proving the work is done.
**The Sentinel Override**: Visual Proof integration. The Foreman takes photos of the installed drywall on their phone. Constructiv appends these GPS and time-stamped visual proofs directly to line-item 14 on the G703 Continuation Sheet. The GC cannot deny the progress because the photographic proof is cryptographically linked to the line item.

## Use Case 3: Prompt Payment Act Triggering
**The Competitor Way**: Sending a G702 via email and waiting 90 days for a check, absorbing the cash flow hit.
**The Sentinel Override**: Constructiv's e-delivery system tracks exactly when the GC opens the invoice. It contains automated logic that flags when the statutory "Prompt Payment" grace period (e.g., 30 days in many states) is violated. It automatically generates a legally formatted "Notice of Intent to Lien" draft if the GC misses the deadline.
