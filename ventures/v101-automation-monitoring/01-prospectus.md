# Venture Prospectus: AutoSentinel (Automation Monitoring)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Silent Failure"

As companies deploy more automation workflows (Zapier, Make, n8n), they face a critical visibility gap. An automation can fail silently for days, causing missed orders, lost leads, and corrupted data.

- **Stats**: 67% of automation practitioners report "undetected failures" as their top pain point.
- **Current State**: Most tools offer basic webhook alerts but no intelligent monitoring, loop detection, or schema drift warnings.

## 🟢 The Solution: AutoSentinel

A real-time observability platform for no-code/low-code automations.

- **Key Innovation**: "Automation Graph Intelligence"—it maps every workflow across platforms and detects issues before they cascade.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **Starter**: $29/mo for individual automators (5 workflows).
- **Pro**: $99/mo for teams (unlimited workflows, Slack alerts).
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing with API access and SSO.

## 🌍 Market Potential

- **TAM**: $8.2B by 2028 (Workflow Automation Market).
- **Urgency**: High. As "Shadow IT" automation grows, so does the need for governance.
