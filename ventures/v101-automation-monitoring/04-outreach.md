# Outreach Strategy: AutoSentinel

## 🎯 Target Personas
- **Automation Consultants**: Managing 20+ client workflows, need visibility.
- **Operations Managers**: Responsible for workflow reliability.
- **Small Business Owners**: Using Zapier/Make but lacking IT oversight.

## 🏢 Target Channels (Pilot Phase)
- **r/nocode**, r/automation, r/zapier communities.
- **LinkedIn** automation-focused groups.
- **Maker communities** (Make, n8n forums).

## 💬 Outreach Scripts
### The "Failure Story" Hook
> "I lost $3,000 in orders because a Zapier workflow silently failed for 3 days. That's when I built AutoSentinel. Want to see if your automations are healthy?"

### The "Free Audit" Offer
> "We'll monitor your top 5 automations for free for 14 days. You'll see exactly how often they fail and why."

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy
- **YouTube**: "How I caught a $10k automation failure before it happened."
- **Product Hunt**: Launch as a free tool for indie hackers.
