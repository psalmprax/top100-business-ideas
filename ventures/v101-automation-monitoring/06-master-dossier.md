# Master Dossier: AutoSentinel

## Executive Summary

AutoSentinel addresses the critical gap in workflow automation governance. As companies deploy more automations across Zapier, Make, and n8n, they face a visibility crisis where failures go undetected for days, causing data loss and financial damage.

## The Opportunity

### Problem Statement

- **67%** of automation practitioners report undetected failures as their top pain point.
- Average cost of silent automation failure: **$2,400** per incident (lost orders, corrupted data, missed leads).
- Market growing **23% CAGR** as more businesses adopt no-code workflows.

### Solution

A dedicated observability platform for automation workflows that provides:

- Real-time failure detection
- Loop and recursion prevention
- Schema drift monitoring
- Visual workflow mapping

### Market Validation

- 5M+ active Zapier users
- 3M+ Make users
- 500K+ n8n self-hosted instances
- Total addressable market: **$8.2B by 2028**
