# Extended Use Cases: PricePoint (Software Price Monitor)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)
### Use Case 1: SaaS Price-Drop Alerts
Monitors pricing pages of major SaaS tools and alerts users when a discount or lower tier is introduced.
### Use Case 2: "Grandfathered" Clause Protection
Alerts users when a tool updates its Terms of Service regarding pricing for existing users, helping them lock in better rates.
### Use Case 3: Alternative Recommendation Engine
Suggests cheaper or free alternatives when a monitored tool crosses a specific price threshold.

---

## Extended Use Cases (4-10)

### Use Case 4: One-Click Price Watch (Self-Service)
**Scenario**: User finds a tool on ProductHunt and wants to monitor its price before buying.
**Solution**: Browser extension that allows the user to mark a pricing page and set a "Target Entry Price" with one click.

### Use Case 5: Finance Team "Negotiation Kit" (Integration)
**Scenario**: High-volume SaaS bill is coming up for renewal.
**Solution**: "Leverage Report" showing the lowest available public pricing, competitor price drops, and "Grandfathered" rate history to use in negotiation.

### Use Case 6: SaaS Inflation Analytics
**Scenario**: Tracking how much total company SaaS spend has increased due to price hikes.
**Solution**: Portfolio-wide insight showing the "Weighted Average Price Hike" across all tools in the stack over the last 12 months.

### Use Case 7: High-Velocity Price Audit (SLA)
**Scenario**: An enterprise IT department with a $10M+ annual SaaS budget needs a verified "Market Price Audit" for their top 50 vendors for an upcoming budget review.
**Solution**: Enterprise-tier PricePoint provides a 4-hour "Price Intelligence" SLA. This guarantees a verified report showing the lowest known contracted rates, public discounts, and negotiation leverage for any enterprise tool in the stack within a critical time window.

### Use Case 8: Mobile "Price-Spike" Notification (Mobile)
**Scenario**: A critical tool suddenly doubles its price (e.g., Unity/Airtable style update) while the CFO is traveling.
**Solution**: Instant, high-priority mobile notifications with biometric lock. The CFO can view the price-impact analysis, see recommended alternative tools, and trigger an "Emergency Evaluation" task for the procurement team directly from the phone.

### Use Case 9: Global Identity & Procurement Access (Security)
**Scenario**: A multinational corporation needs to manage access to proprietary "Vendor Negotiation Kits" and contract rate history across 15 global finance hubs.
**Solution**: Full integration with Enterprise SSO (Okta/Azure AD) and granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). PricePoint ensures that only authorized regional procurement managers can view sensitive contract rate history for their specific vendors.

### Use Case 10: Procurement Compliance & Audit Ledger (Compliance)
**Scenario**: Internal auditors require a "Tamper-Proof" record of every tool's pricing history to prove the company always secured the best available market rate.
**Solution**: Every price-drop alert, negotiation report, and contract rate entry is recorded in an immutable "Procurement Ledger." This provides a cryptographically signed audit trail that satisfies SOC2 and internal financial governance standards.
