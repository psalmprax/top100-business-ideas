# Master Dossier: CostControl

## Executive Summary

CostControl addresses the $23B problem of wasted SaaS spend. Average companies use 187 SaaS apps, with 23% going unused. Finance teams lack visibility into what departments are actually spending.

## The Opportunity

### Problem Statement

- **$23B**: Annual waste on unused SaaS licenses
- **187**: Average SaaS apps per mid-market company
- **23%**: Of SaaS licenses are unused at any given time

### Solution

Automated SaaS governance platform that:

- Detects all SaaS purchases via email/browser scanning
- Correlates usage data with spend
- Identifies waste and optimization opportunities

### Market Validation

- Zylo raised $53M (proof of market)
- BetterCloud valued at $1B+
- Customer demand is proven in CFO forums

## Business Model

- **Free**: Basic scan (5 apps)
- **Starter** ($49/mo): 20 apps tracked
- **Pro** ($149/mo): Unlimited + alerts
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing
