# Product Roadmap: CostControl

## Phase 1: "Shadow IT Detector" (Week 1-4) [MVP]

- **Core Feature**: Browser extension + email scanning to detect SaaS purchases.
- **Dashboard**: List all detected apps with estimated spend.
- **Alerts**: Email notifications for new subscriptions.

## Phase 2: "License Optimizer" (Month 2-3)

- **Login Tracking**: Integrate with SSO (Okta, Google Workspace) to track actual usage.
- **Recommendations**: Suggest license reductions based on login data.
- **Renewal Alerts**: Calendar integration for contract renewals.

## Phase 3: "Governance Suite" (Month 4-6)

- **Approval Workflows**: Require approval for new SaaS purchases.
- **Budget Caps**: Set department-level spending limits.
- **Compliance Reports**: Generate audit-ready spend reports.
