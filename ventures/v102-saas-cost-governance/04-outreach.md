# Outreach Strategy: CostControl

## 🎯 Target Personas

- **CFO/Finance Director**: Concerned about SaaS spend visibility.
- **IT Manager**: Dealing with "Shadow IT" and license management.
- **Procurement**: Managing vendor contracts and renewals.

## 🏢 Target Channels (Pilot Phase)

- **LinkedIn**: Finance and IT procurement groups.
- **r/SaaS**: Reddit community for SaaS discussions.
- **SaaS Optimizer communities**: Slack groups for ops professionals.

## 💬 Outreach Scripts

### The "Audit" Hook

> "We analyzed 50 mid-market companies and found an average of $180K in wasted SaaS spend. Want us to run a free scan of your company's subscriptions?"

### The "Renewal Risk" Approach

> "Your Salesforce contract renews in 90 days. We can help you negotiate better terms with usage data. Interested?"

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy

- **Webinars**: "How to reduce SaaS spend by 30%."
- **Case studies**: Companies that saved $100K+.
- **Partner programs**: IT consultants as resellers.
