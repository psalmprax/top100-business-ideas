# Venture Prospectus: CostControl (SaaS Governance)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The $50,000 Leak"

Companies lose an average of **23% of their SaaS spend** to unused licenses, duplicate tools, and "Shadow IT." Most CFOs have no visibility into what departments are actually spending.

- **Stats**: Average company has 187 SaaS apps; 23% are unused.
- **Current State**: Finance teams rely on manual audits or expensive enterprise tools.

## 🟢 The Solution: CostControl

A SaaS governance platform that automatically detects, tracks, and optimizes software spend.

- **Key Innovation**: "Usage-Based Attribution"—it correlates actual login data with spend to identify waste.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **Starter**: $49/mo for up to 20 apps.
- **Pro**: $149/mo for unlimited apps + alerts.
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing with API access.

## 🌍 Market Potential

- **TAM**: $72B by 2028 (SaaS Management Market).
- **Urgency**: Very High. SaaS sprawl is accelerating post-pandemic.
