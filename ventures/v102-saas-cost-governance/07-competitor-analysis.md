## Competitive Advantage

### Our Wedge: "Usage-Based Optimization"

- Not just tracking, but **actionable recommendations**
- **Login correlation** shows actual usage vs. paid seats
- **Renewal intelligence** for negotiation leverage

### Differentiation Strategy

1. **Mid-market focus**: Perfectly priced between free and enterprise
2. **Email scanning**: Unique data source others don't have
3. **Actionable**: Not just dashboards, but specific recommendations

## Market Positioning

- **Position**: "The CFO's SaaS Copilot"
- **Target**: 50-500 employee companies
- **Message**: "Find $50K in wasted SaaS spend in 15 minutes"

## Defensibility

- Proprietary SaaS pricing database
- SSO integration depth
- Usage attribution methodology
