# Alpha Venture Details: CostControl

## Market Context

### The Problem Evolution

- **2020**: Remote work triggers SaaS explosion.
- **2023**: Companies realize they have "SaaS sprawl" - too many tools.
- **2025**: First "SaaS rationalization" trends emerge.
- **2026**: Market ready for automated governance.

### Target Market Size

- **TAM**: $72B (SaaS Management) by 2028
- **SAM**: $12B (Mid-market SaaS governance)
- **SOM**: $500M (First 3 years)

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor  | Strength         | Weakness               |
| ----------- | ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| Zylo        | Enterprise focus | Expensive; slow        |
| BetterCloud | Feature-rich     | Complex implementation |
| Spendesk    | Spend management | Not SaaS-specific      |

### GTM Strategy

1. **Freemium audit**: Free scan of current SaaS stack.
2. **Finance community**: CFO-focused content marketing.
3. **Partner network**: IT consultants and MSPs.

### Key Metrics

- Companies scanned
- Average waste identified ($)
- Subscription optimization rate

### Defensibility

- Proprietary SaaS pricing database
- Integration depth with SSO providers
- Usage attribution algorithms
