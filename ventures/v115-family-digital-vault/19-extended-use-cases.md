# Extended Use Cases: LegacyLink (Family Digital Vault)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)
### Use Case 1: Shared Password "Emergency" Access
Secure way for family members to access critical accounts only in verified emergency scenarios.
### Use Case 2: Digital Inheritance Map
Documenting which subscriptions and assets (Crypto, Accounts) belong to whom for estate planning.
### Use Case 3: Child Safe-Storage
Shared vault for birth certificates, school records, and health data accessible by both parents.

---

## Extended Use Cases (4-10)

### Use Case 4: Vault "Succession" Setup (Self-Service)
**Scenario**: User wants to ensure their spouse gets access to the vault but not their children yet.
**Solution**: Step-by-step "Succession Wizard" that defines granular access rules based on life events and verified legal documentation.

### Use Case 5: Banking & Crypto Integration
**Scenario**: Linking active bank accounts to track total family holdings.
**Solution**: Secure Open-Banking and Wallet connectors that pull real-time balances into the "Inheritance Overview" without storing raw keys.

### Use Case 6: Family Wealth Projections
**Scenario**: Planning for the child's university or house deposit.
**Solution**: Aggregated dashboard of all family assets (savings, crypto, property) with simple "What-If" growth calculators.

### Use Case 7: Institutional Legal Support (SLA)
**Scenario**: Complex family estate across multiple jurisdictions.
**Solution**: Premium concierge support that connects users with localized legal experts to verify the vault's compliance with regional laws.

### Use Case 8: Mobile "Proof of Life" Check-ins
**Scenario**: Triggering access based on a period of inactivity.
**Solution**: Native mobile "Pulse" that periodically asks for a biometrically verified check-in to confirm the user is active.

### Use Case 9: Quantum-Resistant Encryption (Security)
**Scenario**: Storing ultra-long-term documents (50+ years).
**Solution**: Future-proofed encryption standards designed to resist emerging decryption technologies.

### Use Case 10: Multi-Jurisdictional Mapping (Compliance)
**Scenario**: Family living in the EU but assets held in the US.
**Solution**: Automatically flags if a specific asset/document requires a local "Digital Will" or specific tax filing across borders.
