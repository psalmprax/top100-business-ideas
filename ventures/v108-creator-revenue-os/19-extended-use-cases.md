# Extended Use Cases: CreatorCash (Creator Revenue OS)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)

### Use Case 1: The "Multi-Platform" Revenue Pulse
**The Competitor Way**: You log into YouTube, Patreon, Skool, and TikTok separately to see how much you made this month. You have to manually add it up in a spreadsheet.
**The CreatorCash Override**: CreatorCash aggregates every revenue stream via API. It gives you a real-time "Net Worth of the Brand" dashboard, showing your total earnings across 10+ platforms in a single currency and a single view.

### Use Case 2: Automated Tax-Bucket Splitting
**The Competitor Way**: You get a $5,000 payout from Patreon. You spend it. At the end of the year, you realize you owe $1,500 in tax and don't have the cash.
**The CreatorCash Override**: As soon as a payout hits your connected bank account, CreatorCash auto-calculates your estimated tax liability. It suggests a "Tax Transfer" amount and integrated with your bank to move that money into a separate "Tax Vault" automatically.

### Use Case 3: Sponsorship ROI Tracker
**The Competitor Way**: You do a brand deal for $2,000. You have no idea if the "Conversion" metrics you sent to the brand resulted in more work or if you undercharged.
**The CreatorCash Override**: CreatorCash tracks every sponsorship contract and its "Deliverables" (e.g., links, clicks). it calculates your "Effective CPM" for every deal and suggests a "Current Market Price" for your next negotiation based on actual performance.

## Extended Use Cases (4-10)

### Use Case 4: Affiliate Income Consolidation
**Scenario**: Tracking Amazon, Impact, and ShareASale income
**Solution**: Unified affiliate dashboard showing which specific links are performing best across all networks.

### Use Case 5: Expense Matching (Deductions)
**Scenario**: Buying a new camera ($2k) and needing to deduct it from YouTube income
**Solution**: Automatic matching of hardware/software purchases to your "Creator Category" for tax filing.

### Use Case 6: Subscription Churn Alerts
**Scenario**: Losing high-value Patreon members silently
**Solution**: Real-time alerts when a "VIP" member cancels, with automated "Saved Offer" or "Exit Survey" triggers.

### Use Case 7: Revenue Diversification Score
**Scenario**: 90% of income comes from one platform (High Risk)
**Solution**: A "Risk Score" showing your dependency on a single platform and suggesting new streams (e.g., "Start a newsletter to drop risk to 40%").

### Use Case 8: Native Mobile "Earnings-Pulse" (Mobile)
**Scenario**: Creator wants to check their net earnings across all 10+ platforms during a shoot.
**Solution**: Ultra-fast mobile widget and app providing real-time "Brand Valuation" and instant payout notifications.

### Use Case 9: Multi-Currency Exchange
**Scenario**: Getting paid in USD/EUR while living in the UK
**Solution**: Integrated multi-currency wallet with fair exchange rates and low-fee transfers.

### Use Case 10: Exit/Valuation Estimator
**Scenario**: Wanting to sell your channel/brand
**Solution**: AI-powered valuation based on revenue stability, audience engagement, and platform "Stickiness."
