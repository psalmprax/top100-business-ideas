# Product Roadmap: CreatorCash

## Phase 1: "Dashboard" (Week 1-4) [MVP]
- **Core Feature**: Connect YouTube, Patreon, TikTok APIs.
- **Dashboard**: Unified earnings view.
- **Alerts**: Payment notifications.

## Phase 2: "Tax Tools" (Month 2-3)
- **Tax Estimation**: Auto-calculate tax liability.
- **Receipt Tracking**: Expense categorization.
- **Export**: Tax filing exports.

## Phase 3: "Financial" (Month 4-6)
- **Lending**: Revenue-based loans for creators.
- **Investments**: Savings products.
- **Advisory**: Creator financial planning.
