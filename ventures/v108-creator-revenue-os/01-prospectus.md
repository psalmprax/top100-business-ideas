# Venture Prospectus: CreatorCash (Creator Revenue OS)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Fragmented Income"

Creators earn from YouTube, Patreon, TikTok, sponsorships, and affiliate links—but have no unified view of their income.

- **Stats**: Average creator uses 7+ platforms; 64% use spreadsheets to track.
- **Current State**: No single tool aggregates all revenue streams.

## 🟢 The Solution: CreatorCash

A unified revenue dashboard for creators.

- **Key Innovation**: "Revenue Aggregation"—connects all platforms via APIs for real-time earnings view.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **Free**: 2 platforms connected.
- **Pro** ($19/mo): Unlimited connections + tax tools.
- **Agency** ($99/mo): For manager/creator pairs.

## 🌍 Market Potential

- **TAM**: $1.2B by 2028 (Creator Economy Tools).
