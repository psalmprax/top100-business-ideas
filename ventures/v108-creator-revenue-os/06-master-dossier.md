# Master Dossier: CreatorCash

## Executive Summary

CreatorCash unifies revenue tracking for creators across YouTube, Patreon, TikTok, sponsorships, and affiliate links. It provides a single view of creator income with built-in tax tools.

## The Opportunity

- **TAM**: $1.2B by 2028.
- **Users**: 50M+ creators.
- **Gap**: Fragmented revenue tracking.

## Business Model

- **Free**: 2 platforms.
- **Pro** ($19/mo): Unlimited.
- **Agency** ($99/mo): Managers.

## GTM

1. Creator community.
2. Platform partnerships.
3. Tax tool bundles.

## Risk Assessment

- **Low**: Proven market need.
- **Medium**: Platform API changes.
