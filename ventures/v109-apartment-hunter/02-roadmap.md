# Product Roadmap: RentPulse

## Phase 1: "Instant Alerts" (Week 1-4) [MVP]

- **Core Feature**: Real-time listing monitoring (60-second scans).
- **Alerts**: Push notifications for new listings.
- **Dashboard**: Saved searches and favorites.

## Phase 2: "Auto-Response" (Month 2-3)

- **One-Click Apply**: Automated inquiry sending.
- **Viewing Booking**: Auto-schedule viewings.
- **Renter Profile**: Pre-built applications.

## Phase 3: "Intelligence" (Month 4-6)

- **Price Prediction**: Rent trend analysis.
- **Neighborhood Insights**: Safety, commute data.
- **AI Matching**: Smart listing recommendations.
