# Exit Strategy: RentPulse

## Acquisition Targets

- **Zillow/Redfin**: Would benefit from real-time monitoring tech.
- **Trulia**: Could integrate as premium feature.
- **PropTech startups**: Property management companies.

## Exit Scenarios

1. **Strategic Acquisition**: Larger PropTech company wants our scraping/alert technology.
2. **IPO**: If we reach $10M+ ARR in competitive markets.
3. **Secondary Sale**: To real estate tech-focused PE firms.

## Timeline

- **Short-term (2-3 years)**: Focus on US urban markets.
- **Medium-term (3-5 years)**: Expand internationally.
- **Long-term (5-7 years)**: Potential acquisition or IPO.

## Key Metrics for Exit

- 100K+ active users
- $2M+ ARR
- Proprietary scraping technology
- Strong market position in 5+ cities
