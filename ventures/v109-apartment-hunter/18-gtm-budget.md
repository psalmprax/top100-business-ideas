## Channel Strategy

### Primary Channels
1. **Reddit** (r/NYC, r/LosAngeles, r/ApartmentRentals)
   - Organic posting in housing groups
   - Sponsored posts in target cities
   - Budget: $8,000

2. **SEO/Content**
   - Apartment hunting guides for major cities
   - "How to find apartment in [City]" content
   - Budget: $15,000

3. **University Partnerships**
   - Partner with housing offices
   - Campus ambassador program
   - Budget: $5,000

### Secondary Channels
- **Facebook Groups**: Housing groups in target cities
- **Google Ads**: "apartment alert" keywords
- **Craigslist alternatives**: Apartment listing aggregators
