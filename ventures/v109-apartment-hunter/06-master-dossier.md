# Master Dossier: RentPulse

## Executive Summary
RentPulse provides real-time apartment listing alerts and automated response tools for renters in competitive markets. It addresses the problem of missing ideal apartments due to slow response times.

## The Opportunity
- **TAM**: $1.8B by 2028.
- **Gap**: 67% miss ideal apartments.
- **Trend**: Competitive urban markets.

## Business Model
- **Free**: Limited alerts.
- **Pro** ($9/mo): Unlimited.
- **Premium** ($29/mo): AI + concierge.

## GTM
1. Urban renter communities.
2. Relocation services.
3. University partnerships.

## Risk Assessment
- **Low**: Proven pain point.
- **Medium**: Legal issues with scraping.
