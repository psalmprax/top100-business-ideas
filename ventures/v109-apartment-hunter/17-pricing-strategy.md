# Pricing Strategy: RentPulse

## Pricing Tiers

### Free Tier
- **Price**: $0
- **Features**: 
  - 3 saved searches
  - Email alerts (delayed)
  - Basic dashboard
- **Target**: Casual renters

### Pro Tier
- **Price**: $9.99/month
- **Features**:
  - Unlimited saved searches
  - Real-time push notifications
  - One-click apply automation
  - Mobile app access
- **Target**: Serious apartment hunters

### Premium Tier
- **Price**: $29.99/month
- **Features**:
  - Everything in Pro
  - AI-powered listing recommendations
  - Concierge service (viewing scheduling)
  - Market analysis reports
  - Priority support
- **Target**: Relocating professionals

## Pricing Psychology
- Pro tier at $9.99 (under $10) feels accessible.
- Premium at $29.99 positioned as " VIP" experience.
- Annual discount: 20% off ($95/year vs $120).

## Enterprise/Partnership Pricing
- **Universities**: White-label for housing offices ($500/month)
- **Relocation services**: API access ($1000/month)
- **Property managers**: Listing promotion features
