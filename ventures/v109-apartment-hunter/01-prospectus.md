# Venture Prospectus: RentPulse (Apartment Hunting Automation)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Lost Apartment"

The perfect apartment is listed and rented within hours. By the time you see it, it's gone. Current tools are too slow.

- **Stats**: 67% of renters miss their ideal apartment due to slow response.
- **Current State**: Zillow/Rightmove alerts come 30+ minutes late.

## 🟢 The Solution: RentPulse

Real-time apartment listing alerts and automated response.

- **Key Innovation**: "Listing Sniping"—instant alerts + one-click response.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **Free**: Limited alerts.
- **Pro** ($9/mo): Unlimited alerts + automation.
- **Premium** ($29/mo): AI + Concierge.
