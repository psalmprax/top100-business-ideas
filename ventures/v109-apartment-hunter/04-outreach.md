# Outreach Strategy: RentPulse

## 🎯 Target Personas

- **Urban renters**: Looking for apartments in competitive markets.
- **Relocating professionals**: Moving to new cities.
- **Students**: Looking for housing near campuses.

## 🏢 Target Channels

- **Reddit** r/ApartmentRentals, r/NYC, r/LosAngeles.
- **Facebook** housing groups.
- **Craigslist** alternative communities.

## 💬 Outreach Scripts

### The "Missed Apartment" Hook

> "I missed 3 perfect apartments because I saw them 2 hours too late. That's why I built RentPulse."

### The "Competitive Market" Approach

> "In NYC, apartments last 4 days on the market. We'll alert you in 60 seconds."

## 🔗 Viral Strategy

- **University partnerships**.
- **Relocation guide partnerships**.
- **Referral program**.
