# Outreach Strategy: DenialDefense (Healthcare Capture)

## 🎯 Target Personas
- **Practice Manager**: Dealing with the cash-flow crunch of 25% denial rates.
- **Biller/Coder**: Overwhelmed by volume and constant payer policy changes.
- **Surgical Specialty Doctor**: (Orthopedic, Cardiology) where single denials cost thousands.

## 🏢 Target Channels (Pilot Phase)
- **Specialized Billing Agencies**: They want to increase their own productivity and "Recovery Rates."
- **ASC Associations**: (Ambulatory Surgery Centers) - high volume, high-value, high-denial targets.
- **Healthcare IT Reddit (r/HealthIT / r/MedicalBilling)**: Position as "Defense against Payer AI."

## 💬 Outreach Scripts
### The "AI Defense" Approach
> "Hi [Name], insurance companies are now using AI to reject your claims for minor modifier errors. You need an 'AI Defense' to fight back. We've built DenialDefense—it audits your clinical notes *before* you submit, scoring the denial risk and suggesting the correct modifiers in real-time. We're looking for 3 specialized surgical clinics to trial a 30-day 'Denial Audit' where we identify how much revenue you're currently leaving on the table. Interested?"

### The "Coder Shortage" Approach
> "Hi [Name], it's getting impossible to find experienced coders who can keep up with payer changes. We've automated 80% of the manual coding process with an AI that specifically targets 'Denial Prevention'. Can I show you our EHR overlay that helps your current team code 3x faster with zero errors?"

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy
- **Webinars**: "The 5 Modifiers Costing You $50k/Year"—Educational content showing common payer 'traps'.
- **Case Study**: "How [Clinic X] reduced initial denials by 40% in 60 days."
