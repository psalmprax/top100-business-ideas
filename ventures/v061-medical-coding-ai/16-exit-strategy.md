## 🛤️ Exit Roadmap

### Phase 1: Prove the Accuracy (Years 1-2)
- [ ] Win 3 major University Research Hospitals.
- [ ] Publish peer-reviewed studies proving MediParse increases coding accuracy by 12% and reduces DNFB by 4 days.
- **Target**: Build unassailable clinical credibility. We aren't "tech bros," we are clinically rigorous.

### Phase 2: The Network Effect (Years 3-4)
- [ ] Become the highest-rated Revenue Cycle app on the Epic Showroom.
- [ ] Process 5 Million patient charts globally per month.
- **Target**: Force the legacy players (3M, Optum) to the negotiating table by aggressively eating their market share at renewals.
