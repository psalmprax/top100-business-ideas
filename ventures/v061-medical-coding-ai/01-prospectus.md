# Venture Prospectus: DenialDefense (AI Medical Coding)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The AI Auto-Denial Arms Race"
Insurance payers (UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, etc.) are deploying AI to "Auto-Deny" claims for minor documentation mismatches. 
- **The Impact**: Healthcare providers lose $935B annually to billing errors and denials. Specialized clinics are seeing a 20%+ increase in initial denial rates.
- **The Gap**: Most Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) software is retrospective—it tells you *why* you were denied *after* the loss. Doctors need a "Defense AI" that ensures the claim is "Denial-Proof" *before* submission.

## 🟢 The Solution: DenialDefense
An AI-powered pre-submission auditor for specialized medical clinics. It scans clinical notes, identifies the optimal ICD-10/CPT codes, and cross-references them against the *current* denial algorithms of major payers.
- **Key Innovation**: "Payer-Specific Adaptation"—the engine learns that "Payer A" denies "Modifier 59" on Tuesdays but accepts it on Wednesdays, adjusting the submission strategy in real-time.

## 💰 Revenue Model
- **Performance-Based**: 5% of "Recovered Revenue" from prevented denials.
- **SaaS Tier**: $999/mo for small specialized clinics.

## 🌍 Market Potential
- **TAM**: $100B+ (US Medical Billing and RCM market).
- **Urgency**: Critical. There is a shortage of 200,000+ human medical coders.
