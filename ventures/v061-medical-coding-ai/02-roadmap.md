# Product Roadmap: DenialDefense

## Phase 1: "The Pre-Submit Auditor" (Week 1-4) [MVP]
- **Core Feature**: Upload a clinical note; AI returns the most likely 3-5 CPT/ICD-10 codes.
- **Denial Warning**: Flag "High-Risk" combinations that likely trigger manual reviews.
- **Integration**: Lite-sync with AthenaHealth or DrChrono via browser extension.

## Phase 2: "The Modifier Master" (Month 2-3)
- **Advanced Logic**: Automated application of Modifiers (25, 50, 59) which are the #1 cause of denials.
- **Payer Profiles**: Basic database of "Denial Moods" for Top-5 US Payers.
- **Batch Processing**: Scans the entire day's clinic notes for billing readiness.

## Phase 3: "Autonomous RCM" (Month 4-6)
- **Direct Submission**: Connect to Clearinghouses (Change Healthcare, Waystar).
- **Appeal Bot**: Automatically generates formal appeal letters for the 10% of claims that still get denied.
- **Defense-as-a-Service**: White-label for existing billing agencies.
