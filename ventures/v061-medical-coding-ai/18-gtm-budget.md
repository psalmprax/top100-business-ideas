# GTM Budget & Strategy: MediParse (Medical Coding AI)

## 📊 GTM Strategy Overview

Selling to hospital systems involves arguably the longest, most brutal sales cycles in B2B software (often 12-18 months).

Our GTM relies entirely on **Enterprise "Whale Hunting"**, **Trade Shows**, and **EHR App Store Partnerships**. We will spend £0 on Facebook Ads or generic content marketing.

### Channel Mix (Year 1)

| Channel                   | % of Budget | Monthly Spend | Core Activity                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Major Trade Shows         | 50%         | £12,500       | Sponsoring massive booths at HIMSS, HLTH, and HFMA (Healthcare Financial Management Assoc). |
| Outbound Enterprise AEs   | 30%         | £7,500        | T&E budget for flying veteran salespeople to meet Hospital CFOs face-to-face.               |
| EHR Partnership Marketing | 10%         | £2,500        | Paying for premium placement in the Epic App Orchard and Cerner Code.                       |
| Pilot Subsidies           | 10%         | £2,500        | "Loss Leaders": Doing the first 30 days for a hospital for free just to prove the ROI.      |

## 🎯 Channel Strategies

### Channel 1: The "Free Audit" Wedge (Direct Outbound)

Hospital CFOs do not believe software demos. They only believe data.
| Action | Execution |
|----------|----------|
| The Pitch | "Give us 500 patient charts from last year that have _already been billed and closed_. Give us read-only access." |
| The Proof | We run the 500 charts through MediParse overnight. |
| The Close | We hand the CFO a report: "Your human team missed £45,000 in secondary diagnoses across these 500 charts. The software pays for itself." |

### Channel 2: Healthcare Financial Trade Shows (HFMA)

We ignore "Tech" conferences. We go where the Hospital CFOs go to complain about cash flow.
| Target | Execution | Expected ROI |
|----------|----------|--------|
| HFMA Annual Conference | Sponsor a closed-door roundtable discussion on "Solving the DNFB Backlog Crisis." | Secure 5 enterprise pilots per conference. |

### Channel 3: The Medical Billing Company Flip

Instead of fighting the human BPO billing companies, we arm them.
| Strategy | Execution |
|----------|----------|
| Target | Mid-sized Medical Billing Agencies (the outsourced companies that handle billing for 10-20 regional hospitals). |
| The Pitch | "White-label our AI. Your human coders will suddenly be able to process 10x as many charts, increasing your margins from 20% to 60% instantly." |
