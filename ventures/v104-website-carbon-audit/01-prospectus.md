# Venture Prospectus: GreenMetrics (Website Carbon Audit)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Digital Carbon Footprint"

Websites consume energy through hosting, data transfer, and script execution. Companies are under pressure to report digital carbon emissions for ESG compliance, but have no way to measure it.

- **Stats**: Average website produces 1.6g CO2 per page view.
- **Current State**: No standard measurement tool exists.

## 🟢 The Solution: GreenMetrics

A website carbon audit tool that calculates the environmental impact of web properties.

- **Key Innovation**: "Full-Stack Carbon Analysis"—measures hosting, transfer, and client-side execution.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **Free**: Basic scan for 1 website.
- **Pro** ($29/mo): Unlimited scans + reports.
- **Enterprise**: Custom reporting for CSRD compliance.

## 🌍 Market Potential

- **TAM**: $2.1B by 2028 (ESG Tech Market).
- **Urgency**: Growing with ESG regulations.
