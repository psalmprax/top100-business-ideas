# Outreach Strategy: GreenMetrics

## 🎯 Target Personas

- **Sustainability Officers**: ESG reporting responsibilities.
- **Web Developers**: Want to build green websites.
- **Marketing**: Need green credentials for branding.

## 🏢 Target Channels (Pilot Phase)

- **r/webdev**, r/sustainability communities.
- **LinkedIn** sustainability groups.
- **Green tech conferences**.

## 💬 Outreach Scripts

### The "ESG Compliance" Hook

> "CSRD requires digital carbon reporting. We'll audit your website's carbon footprint in 5 minutes—free."

### The "SEO + Green" Approach

> "Google rewards fast, green websites. Our audit shows how to improve both your SEO and carbon footprint."

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy

- **Eco-influencer partnerships**.
- **Green web design awards**.
- **Case studies with measurable impact**.
