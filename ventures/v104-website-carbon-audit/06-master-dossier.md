# Master Dossier: GreenMetrics

## Executive Summary
GreenMetrics addresses the emerging need for website carbon measurement. With ESG regulations (CSRD) requiring digital footprint reporting, companies need a way to measure and optimize their web carbon emissions.

## The Opportunity
- **TAM**: $2.1B by 2028.
- **Driver**: EU CSRD requires digital carbon reporting.
- **Gap**: No comprehensive SaaS solution exists.

## Business Model
- **Free**: 1 website scan.
- **Pro** ($29/mo): Unlimited scans.
- **Enterprise**: Custom reporting.

## GTM
1. Free audits for SEO/green credentials.
2. Web agency partnerships.
3. ESG software integrations.

## Risk Assessment
- **Medium**: Emerging market, uncertain timing.
- **Low**: Minimal development cost.
