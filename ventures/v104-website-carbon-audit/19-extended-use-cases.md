# Extended Use Cases: GreenMetrics (Website Carbon Audit)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)

### Use Case 1: The "Digital Dirty" Score
**The Competitor Way**: You look at a generic ESG report that says your company's carbon footprint is "High." You have no idea if your corporate website is contributing to it.
**The GreenMetrics Override**: GreenMetrics scans your entire DOM and network requests. It calculates the *exact* energy cost per page load based on hosting, data transfer, and script execution. It gives you a "Digital Dirty" score and a roadmap to reduce it by 40% using image optimization and server-side rendering.

### Use Case 2: Green-Hosting Verification
**The Competitor Way**: Your hosting provider says they are "Powered by 100% Renewable Energy." You take their word for it.
**The GreenMetrics Override**: GreenMetrics traces your server IPs and cross-references them with global green energy grid data. It verifies the provider's claims and generates a "Verified Green" badge for your footer, protecting you from "Greenwashing" accusations.

### Use Case 3: The "Eco-Budget" Threshold
**The Competitor Way**: A developer adds a 5MB video to the homepage. The page load time increases, but nobody knows the carbon impact.
**The GreenMetrics Override**: GreenMetrics sets an "Eco-Budget" in your CI/CD pipeline. If a PR increases the page's carbon footprint by more than 5%, it flags the build and suggests alternatives (e.g., "Use WebP or lighter CSS").

## Extended Use Cases (4-10)

### Use Case 4: SEO + Carbon Analysis
**Scenario**: Heavy JS is hurting both SEO and the planet
**Solution**: Combined report showing how carbon reduction (less JS/CSS) directly improves Google Core Web Vitals.

### Use Case 5: Regional Grid Impact
**Scenario**: Users in Germany use coal-heavy power; users in Norway use hydro
**Solution**: Dynamic dashboard showing carbon impact based on *where* your users are browsing from.

### Use Case 6: Third-Party Script Audit
**Scenario**: Ad-scripts and trackers are draining battery and energy
**Solution**: Audit of third-party scripts showing which ones are the "Energy Vampires" of your site.

### Use Case 7: High-Volume Audit Support (SLA)
**Scenario**: An enterprise with 5,000+ domains needs an urgent "Digital Carbon Audit" for a sustainability report due in 24 hours.
**Solution**: Enterprise-tier GreenMetrics includes an "Audit Velocity" SLA. This provides dedicated compute lanes for batch-scanning thousands of URLs simultaneously, ensuring full report generation within a 4-hour window regardless of the current queue depth.

### Use Case 8: Mobile "Green-Scout" Site Scan (Mobile)
**Scenario**: A salesperson or sustainability officer is visiting a client and wants to show their website's carbon score instantly.
**Solution**: Lightweight mobile app that performs a "Sustainability Snapshot" on any URL. It generates a shareable PDF report with immediate "Energy Savings" recommendations that can be emailed to the client from the mobile device.

### Use Case 9: Global Identity & Fleet Access (Security)
**Scenario**: A multinational corporation needs to manage auditor access and permission levels across 10 global regions and hundreds of microsites.
**Solution**: Full integration with Enterprise SSO (Okta/Azure AD). GreenMetrics allows for granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), ensuring that only regional sustainability managers can access and edit their specific "Web Fleet" data.

### Use Case 10: CSRD-Compliant Audit Ledger (Compliance)
**Scenario**: Regulatory auditors require a "Tamper-Proof" record of the company's digital carbon footprint for the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD).
**Solution**: Every audit, optimization action, and verification result is recorded in a cryptographically signed "Sustainability Ledger." This provides an immutable audit trail that satisfies EU CSRD and SEC environmental disclosure requirements.
