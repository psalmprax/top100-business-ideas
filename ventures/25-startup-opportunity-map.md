## Strategic Clustering & Pattern Discovery

1. **Mandatory Costs (High WTP)**: Compliance (ReguLens) and Revenue Recovery (DenialDefense) are the strongest plays because they are financially unavoidable.
2. **Actionable Outcomes**: The market is shifting from "Summarization" to "Automation." Users don't want to read; they want the work finished (TaskFlow AI).
3. **Infrastructure Reliability**: As businesses move to agentic workflows, the "Self-Healing/Monitoring" layer (Agent Ops Sentinel) becomes critical.
