# Hiring Roadmap: ReguLens AI

## 🎯 Hiring Philosophy

### Core Values

1. **Regulatory Precision** - We don't guess at the law. We code exactly to the text of the EU AI Act.
2. **Enterprise Polish** - Our buyers are C-Suite and Legal Partners. The product must feel like a premium, secure enterprise vault.

### First 5 Hires

| Priority | Role               | Impact                                                                            | Timing  |
| -------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| 1        | "Legal Engineer"   | Someone with a law degree who can code. They translate Article 14 into SQL logic. | Month 1 |

## 👥 Role Specifications

### Role: Legal Engineer (Domain Expert)

**Department**: Product
**Location**: EU / UK

**Responsibilities**:

- Read the 450+ pages of the EU AI Act texts and secondary standards (CEN/CENELEC).
- Map legal requirements to technical database fields required for the MVP.
- Work closely with engineering to ensure the generated PDF meets strict Annex IV requirements.

**Requirements**:

- Dual background in Law (LLB/LLM) and Technology/Computer Science.
- Previous experience in GDPR/Privacy Tech or GRC software.
