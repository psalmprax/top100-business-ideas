| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |

| Handshake Timeout     | Continuous retry every 60s for the Sentinel bridge.          | Total loss of connection to AgentOps > 1 hour.    |

3. **Data Residency**: Strictly enforce storage of audit metadata within the designated EU-central region.
