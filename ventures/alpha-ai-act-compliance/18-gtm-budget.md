# GTM Budget & Strategy: ReguLens AI

## 📊 GTM Strategy Overview

Selling compliance to developers is impossible (they hate red tape). Selling technical integrations to lawyers is impossible (they don't know what an API is). We must execute a **"Pincer Movement"**. We market the *Fear/Legal* aspects to the DPO, and the *Developer Experience (DX)* to the CTO.

### Channel Mix (Year 1 - The Pre-Deadline Sprint)
| Channel | % of Budget | Monthly Spend | Core Activity |
|---------|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| Partnerships (Law) | 40% | £6,000 | Wine & dine partners at Mid-Tier Tech Law firms. |

## 🎯 Channel Strategies

### Channel 1: Law Firm Partnerships (The Kingmaker)
This is the single most important GTM motion.
| Action | Execution | Expected ROI |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Target Tier-2 Firms | Not Bird & Bird (too slow). Target agile, tech-focused boutiques (e.g., Taylor Wessing, Fieldfisher). | 1 Firm = Access to 50 High-Risk SaaS clients. |
| The Pitch | "You audit the GDPR. Use our software to audit the AI Act. You keep 80% of the consulting margin." | Massive scalable distribution. |

### Channel 2: LinkedIn Ads (Fear & Urgency)
| Audience | Ad Copy | Lead Magnet |
