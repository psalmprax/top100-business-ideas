# Venture Prospectus: AI Act Compliance Suite

## 🔴 The Problem: "The SME Compliance Gap"
The EU AI Act mandates strict "Conformity Assessments" for high-risk AI models (Annex III) by August 2026. 
- **The Cost**: SMEs face **€52,000 per model** in annual compliance costs.
- **The Confusion**: 68% of companies find the 450-page Act "uninterpretable."
- **The Risk**: Fines up to 7% of global turnover.

## 🟢 The Solution: ReguLens AI
A "Conformity Assessment-as-a-SaaS" platform. It guides SMEs through the 10 core requirements (Training Data Bias, Human Oversight, Technical Logs) and generates an audit-ready "Technical Folder" (Annex IV) automatically.
- **Key Innovation**: "Evidence Mapping"—automatically scanning your GitHub/Jira/Model-logs to prove you are meeting safety requirements.

## 💰 Revenue Model
- **Self-Service**: €2,500 per assessment (One-time export).
- **Compliance Monitoring (SaaS)**: €499/mo for Post-Market Monitoring (Article 61 compliance).

## 🌍 Market Potential
- **TAM**: Thousands of EU mid-market firms in Fintech, HR, and Critical Infrastructure.
- **Urgency**: Fixed hard-deadline (August 2026).
