## 🏗️ Part 2: Top-Tier Validated Core Ventures
These are the foundational opportunities ranked for high TAM and specific regulatory wedges.

- **Sector**: ConstructionTech
- **Crisis**: 60-90 day payment delays due to minor clerical errors in G702/G703 documents.
- **The Wedge**: State-specific Prompt Payment Acts requiring GCs to pay error-free docs faster.
- **Mission**: Automate AIA progress billing and linked Lien Waivers.

### 5. Tax-Centric Neobank for EU/UK Freelancers (#002)

- **Crisis**: "Tax Anxiety" among 2M+ UK freelancers facing the **MTD Phase 2 (2026)** mandate.
- **The Wedge**: Mandatory digital bookkeeping for HMRC by April 2026.
- **Mission**: A banking platform where Tax-Provisioning is a core real-time ledger feature.

### 6. EU CSRD-ready ESG Reporting for Mid-Market (#064)
- **Sector**: CleanTech / RegTech
- **Crisis**: EU mid-market companies forced by large clients to provide audit-ready ESG data.
- **The Wedge**: "Timeline Compression"—Mid-market firms must report but lack Big 4 budgets.
- **Mission**: All-in-one software for Double Materiality and auditable Supply Chain data.

### 7. AI-Powered Medical Coding & Revenue Cycle (#061)

- **Crisis**: Payers using "Auto-Denial AI" to reject claims. Doctors lose clinical time to admin.
- **The Wedge**: CMS Mandates (2026) for Digital Prior Authorization.
- **Mission**: "Defense AI" to ensure documentation perfectly aligns with payer algorithms to prevent denials.
