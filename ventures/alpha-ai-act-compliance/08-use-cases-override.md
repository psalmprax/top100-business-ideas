# Competitive Override Use Cases: ReguLens AI Act Compliance

Incumbents in Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) like OneTrust or Vanta treat the EU AI Act like just another generic checklist or privacy law. **ReguLens** is hyper-specialized for the specific technical demands of the August 2026 Annex III regulations.

Here are the specific use cases where ReguLens surpasses the competition:

## Use Case 1: Automated Annex IV Technical Folder Generation
**The Competitor Way**: Hiring a legal consultant for €30,000 to interview your engineers and write a 100-page Word document, or using generic GRC software that just asks "Do you have documentation?"
**The Sentinel Override**: ReguLens integrates with your GitHub and Jira. It automatically extracts your model architecture diagrams, your test coverage reports, and your data lineage tags, and programmatically compiles them into the exact EU-mandated PDF format for the Annex IV "Technical File."

## Use Case 2: The "10-Minute" Training Data Bias Scan (Article 10)
**The Competitor Way**: Deploying massive, expensive MLOps suites (like Credo AI) that require weeks of integration into your training cluster just to evaluate fairness.
**The Sentinel Override**: A secure, isolated "Quick-Scan" tool. A data scientist uploads the metadata (headers and distributions, not the raw PII) of a Parquet/CSV file. ReguLens instantly flags demographic imbalances or missing cohorts required by Article 10 of the AI Act, *before* the costly model training even begins.

## Use Case 3: The "August 2026 Sandbox" (National Authority Audit Prep)
**The Competitor Way**: Waiting until a National Competent Authority (like the CNIL in France or the ICO in the UK) knocks on the door and hoping your paperwork holds up.
**The Sentinel Override**: ReguLens provides an "Adversarial Audit Bot." It simulates an EU inspector. It quizzes your designated DPO (Data Protection Officer) and attempts to find holes in your human-oversight protocols (Article 14). It gives you a "Readiness Score" based on actual expected audit scenarios.
