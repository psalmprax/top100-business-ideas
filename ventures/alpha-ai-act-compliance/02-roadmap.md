# Product Roadmap: ReguLens AI

## Phase 1: "Gap Analysis Engine" (Week 1-3) [MVP]

- **Core Feature**: Article 6 classification wizard (is my AI high-risk?).
- **Internal Audit**: A checklist-based scoring system based on EFRAG/EU standards.
- **Output**: A "Compliance Gap Report" to sell as a lead magnet.

## Phase 2: "Annex IV Technical Folder" (Month 1-3)

- **Document Generation**: Automated templates for Annex IV (Technical Documentation).
- **Data Governance**: Tools to audit training data for "Bias & Completeness" (Article 10).
- **User Portal**: Collaborative space for DPOs (Data Protection Officers) and Legal.

## Phase 3: "Post-Market Sentinel" (Month 4-6)

- **Monitoring**: Article 61 compliant real-time monitoring of deployed models.
- **Incident Reporting**: Templated reporting for serious incidents to National Authorities.
- **API integrations**: Connect to HuggingFace/VertexAI for automatic bias checks.
