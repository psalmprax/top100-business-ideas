# Pricing Strategy: ReguLens AI

## 💰 Pricing Philosophy

### Core Principles
1. **Anchor Against the Fine**: The alternative to our software is a potential fine of €35 Million or 7% of global revenue. £18,000/year for software is a rounding error.
2. **Anchor Against Consultants**: The alternative to our software is hiring a law firm for €50,000 to do a manual audit. We save them €32,000 immediately.
3. **No Free Tier**: Compliance is binary. You are either compliant or you aren't. We do not offer free trials of regulatory safety; we offer paid Proofs of Concept (POCs).

## 💵 Unit Economics by Tier

### The "White-Label" Channel Pricing
Law firms don't want to pay £1,500/mo. We sell Law Firms a **Master License**:
- They pay us £50,000 / year.
- They get 50 "Client Seats" to use with their clients.
- They mark up the cost of the software inside their consulting fee, ensuring everyone wins.

| Tier | Estimated ARPU | Gross Margin |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| Starter | £6,000 / yr | 95% |
| Mid-Market | £18,000 / yr | 90% |

## 💳 Billing Strategy

### B2B Annual Invoicing
Compliance software is procured from the annual Risk/Legal budget. We do not accept monthly credit card payments.
- **100% Annual Upfront Invoicing via Bank Transfer / ACH.**
- This provides massive cash flow to fund engineering and negates standard SaaS churn models, as DPOs will not rip-and-replace compliance software right before an audit.
