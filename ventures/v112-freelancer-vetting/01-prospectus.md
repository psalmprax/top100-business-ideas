# Venture Prospectus: Vetted (Freelancer Vetting)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Hire from Hell"
Hiring freelancers is a gamble. You don't know if their reviews are real or if their work is actually theirs.
- **Stats**: 67% of companies have been burned by a bad freelancer hire.
- **Current State**: Reviews on Upwork/Fiverr can be faked.

## 🟢 The Solution: Vetted
AI-powered freelancer verification platform.
- **Key Innovation**: "Skills Audit"—verify abilities through practical tests.

## 💰 Revenue Model
- **Free**: Basic profile.
- **Vetted** ($29/test): Skills verification.
- **Business** ($99/mo): Unlimited hires with vetting.

## 🌍 Market Potential
- **TAM**: $1.5B by 2028 (Freelancer Platforms).
