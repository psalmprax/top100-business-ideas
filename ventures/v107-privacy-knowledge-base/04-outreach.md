# Outreach Strategy: CryptaVault

## 🎯 Target Personas
- **Privacy advocates**: Already concerned about cloud data.
- **Security professionals**: Need secure note-taking.
- **Enterprise**: GDPR/compliance requirements.

## 🏢 Target Channels
- **r/privacy**, r/privacytools communities.
- **LinkedIn** security groups.
- **Privacy conferences**.

> "Every note in Notion is training their AI. Your private business ideas aren't private anymore."

### The "Compliance" Approach
> "GDPR requires data control. CryptaVault keeps your knowledge completely local."
