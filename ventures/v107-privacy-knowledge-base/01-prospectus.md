# Venture Prospectus: CryptaVault (Privacy-First Knowledge Base)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Cloud Leak"
Every note you put in Notion, Evernote, or Obsidian gets uploaded to company servers. Your private thoughts, business ideas, and personal data are accessible to others.
- **Stats**: 67% of knowledge workers are concerned about data privacy in note apps.
- **Current State**: All mainstream note apps send data to the cloud.

## 🟢 The Solution: CryptaVault
A privacy-first knowledge base that runs AI locally.
- **Key Innovation**: "On-Device AI"—all processing happens on your machine, data never leaves.

## 💰 Revenue Model
- **Free**: Local-only mode.
- **Pro** ($19/mo): Cross-device sync with E2E encryption.
- **Teams** ($49/user): Enterprise security.

## 🌍 Market Potential
- **TAM**: $4.8B by 2028 (Knowledge Management Market).
