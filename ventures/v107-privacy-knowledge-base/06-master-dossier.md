# Master Dossier: CryptaVault

## Executive Summary
CryptaVault is a privacy-first knowledge base with built-in AI. All data stays local; AI runs on-device. This addresses growing privacy concerns about cloud-based note apps.

## The Opportunity
- **TAM**: $4.8B by 2028.
- **Trend**: Privacy awareness increasing.
- **Gap**: No local AI knowledge tools.

## Business Model
- **Free**: Local-only.
- **Pro** ($19/mo): E2E sync.
- **Teams** ($49/user): Enterprise.

## GTM
1. Privacy community.
2. Open source launch.
3. Enterprise compliance.

## Risk Assessment
- **Low**: Growing privacy market.
- **Medium**: Big players could add features.
