# Venture Prospectus: LedgerBank (Tax-Centric Neobank)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The April 2026 Cliff"

UK freelancers and sole traders face a mandatory digital bookkeeping deadline (Making Tax Digital - MTD) in April 2026.

- **The Anxiety**: Freelancers are forced to use clunky accounting software (Xero/QuickBooks) plus a bank account. They constantly "fear" the tax bill because they don't know their real-time liability.
- **The Gap**: Banks like Revolut/Monzo have "Tax Pots," but they are just simple savings buckets. They don't calculate HMRC-compliant tax liability in real-time.

## 🟢 The Solution: LedgerBank

A "Tax-First" banking platform for freelancers. Every transaction is automatically reconciled against current UK/EU tax laws.

- **Key Innovation**: "The Virtual Tax Auditor"—the bank account _is_ the tax return. By April 2026, the user clicks "Submit" in the banking app, and the quarterly MTD update is sent directly to HMRC.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **Subscription**: £15/mo "Pro" plan for full MTD automation.
- **Lending Wedge**: Offer "Tax Loans" to cover the dual 'Payment on Account' bills based on verified transaction history.

## 🌍 Market Potential

- **TAM**: 2M+ UK freelancers + millions of EU sole traders.
- **Urgency**: MTD Phase 2 (Income Tax) deadline: April 2026.
