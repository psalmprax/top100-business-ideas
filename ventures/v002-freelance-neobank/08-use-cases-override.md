# Competitive Override Use Cases: LedgerBank (Freelance MTD)

Retail Neobanks (Monzo, Starling, Revolut) added business accounts as an afterthought, providing simple "Tax Pots" to set money aside. Accounting software (Xero, Quickbooks) is too complex for a single-person graphic designer. **LedgerBank** merges the two for the upcoming 2026 MTD mandate.

Here are the specific use cases where LedgerBank surpasses the competition:

## Use Case 1: Real-Time MTD ITSA Projection
**The Competitor Way**: A freelancer guesses they owe 20% in tax, manually transferring money to a Monzo "Vault." At the end of the year, their accountant logs into Xero and tells them they miscalculated their personal allowance and actually owe £3,000 more.
**The Sentinel Override**: "The Tax-First Ledger." Every time a client pays a £5,000 invoice, LedgerBank instantly parses the transaction, applies the specific UK Income Tax bands (and National Insurance) based on the *exact* YTD profit, and automatically moves the precise liability amount to a locked HMRC-ready reserve.

## Use Case 2: Zero-Touch MTD Quarterly Filing
**The Competitor Way**: Scrambling 4 times a year to find receipts, reconcile banking data with accounting software, and pay an accountant £300 to file the mandated MTD quarterly update.
**The Sentinel Override**: The bank *is* the accounting engine. Because every transaction was categorized natively on the debit card rail, LedgerBank simply prompts the user: "Your Q2 MTD Update is ready. Tap to submit to HMRC." The API connection to HMRC handles the filing instantly.

## Use Case 3: The "Payment on Account" Tax Loan
**The Competitor Way**: Applying for a high-interest Business Loan from a traditional bank to cover a sudden, unexpected tax bill in January.
**The Sentinel Override**: LedgerBank has perfect visibility into the user's historical income and upcoming invoice payouts. If the user's HMRC reserve is short for the January 31st "Payment on Account" deadline, LedgerBank auto-offers a 30-day, low-interest overdraft specifically ring-fenced to pay HMRC directly, secured against verified unpaid invoices.
