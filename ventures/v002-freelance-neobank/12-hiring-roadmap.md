# Hiring Roadmap: SoloFi (Freelance Neobank)

## 🎯 Hiring Philosophy

### Core Values
1. **Consumer Delight** - We are competing with Monzo and Apple Card. The UI must be buttery smooth, fun, and flawless.
2. **Defensive Engineering** - A bug that crashes a photo app is annoying. A bug that duplicates a £500 transaction destroys the company. Paranoia is required.
3. **Asynchronous Default** - Because our users are freelancers working odd hours, our internal culture should mirror them.

### First 5 Hires
| Priority | Role | Impact | Timing |
|----------|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Senior iOS/React Native Dev| Building the primary consumer interface. The app *is* the product. | Month 1 |
| 2 | Backend Eng (FinTech) | Writing the Go/Node microservices that talk to the BaaS provider (Stripe/ClearBank). | Month 1 |
| 3 | Head of Risk & Compliance | Required by the BaaS provider before we can launch. Approves onboarding flows and AML models. | Month 3 |
| 4 | Product Designer | Designing the physical debit card, the packaging, and the app interactions. | Month 3 |
| 5 | Growth Marketer | Designing the viral referral loop ("Give £50, Get £50") and community strategy. | Month 5 |

## 👥 Role Specifications

### Role: Head of Risk & Compliance (MLRO - Money Laundering Reporting Officer)
**Department**: Operations/Legal

**Responsibilities**:
- Own the relationship with our sponsor bank.
- Review flagged accounts and file Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) with the government.
- Tune the automated fraud-detection rules to balance preventing scams with preventing false-positives (which infuriate real users).

**Requirements**:
- 7+ years in FinTech compliance.
- Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist (CAMS).

### Equity Philosophy
Consumer FinTech generally requires massive Series A/B funding rounds, which causes dilution.
- We grant options generous out of the gate (1.0% - 1.5% for founding engineers).
- Four-year vesting, one-year cliff.
