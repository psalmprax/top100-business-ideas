# Exit Strategy: SoloFi (Freelance Neobank)

## 🎯 Exit Options

### Potential Exit Routes
| Option | Timeline | Probability | Value Range |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Acquisition (FinTech/Accounting)** | 4-6 years | 50% | £150M - £500M |
| **Acquisition (Mega-Bank)** | 5-7 years | 20% | £300M - £800M |
| **IPO (Massive Scale)** | 8-10 years| 5% | £2B+ |
| **Fail / Asset Sale** | 1-3 years | 25% | £5M - £10M |

---

## 🏢 Strategic Acquirers

### Primary Targets (Accounting & Payment Behemoths)
| Company | Reason | 
|---------|--------|
| **Intuit (QuickBooks)** | They acquired massive consumer footprint with Mint Space/CreditKarma. They want to completely own the flow of funds for SMBs, not just record them. We are the perfect frontend holding account. |
| **Block (Square / CashApp)** | They understand the micro-merchant perfectly. SoloFi bridges the gap between their consumer CashApp and their merchant Square terminals. |
| **Xero** | Similar thesis to Intuit. They want to move from passive ledger to active bank. |

### Secondary Targets (Legacy Banking)
| Company | Reason | 
|---------|--------|
| **Chase / JP Morgan**| They struggle to acquire Gen-Z and Millennial freelancers because their UX is archaic. They will buy SoloFi to acquire 500,000 active, growing micro-businesses before they mature into mid-market companies. |
| **Capital One** | Aggressive acquirers of consumer tech (e.g., Paribus, Wikibuy). |

## 📊 Valuation Framework

### Valuation Multiples
| Stage | Multiple | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Sub-Scale (Under 100k Users)| 10x-15x Revenue | Valued primarily on the tech stack and the team (Acqui-hire territory). |
| At Scale (500k+ Active Users)| Per-User Premium | Consumer FinTechs are often valued purely on active users. e.g., £500 to £1,000 *per active user*. If we have 1M users, we are a Unicorn (£1 Billion). |

---

## 🛤️ Exit Roadmap

### Phase 1: Prove the "Tax Vault" Wedge (Years 1-2)
- [ ] Onboard 100,000 freelancers.
- [ ] Prove that 70% of them use SoloFi as their *Primary* account (where clients deposit funds directly).
- **Target**: Prove to Intuit that we have captured the top of the funnel.

### Phase 2: Grow Deposit Base & ARPU (Years 3-4)
- [ ] Achieve £500M in customer deposits.
- [ ] Drive ARPU from £15/mo to £40/mo by launching credit products or subscription SaaS tiers.
- **Target**: Become too expensive to compete with, forcing a mega-bank to just buy the portfolio.

## 📈 Value Drivers

### What Increases Exit Value
| Driver | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **Deposit Stickiness** | +++ (If users keep high balances, we (or an acquirer) can lend that money out on the back-end via Net Interest Margin). |
| **Primary Account Status** | +++ (We are useless if we are just a side-card used for coffee. We *must* be the routing number where the £5,000 contractor invoice lands). |
| **Demographic Capture** | ++ (Banks pay a massive premium to acquire Gen-Z software developers and designers). |
