# GTM Budget & Strategy: SoloFi (Freelance Neobank)

## 📊 GTM Strategy Overview

Consumer FinTech is a graveyard of startups that spent £100 to acquire a user worth £50. **We cannot rely on Performance Marketing (Facebook/Google Ads).**

Our entire GTM is based on **Community Validation**, **Influencer Networks (FinTwit & TikTok)**, and a **Viral Referral Engine**.

| Channel              | % of Budget | Monthly Spend | Core Activity                                                 |
| -------------------- | ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |

## 🎯 Channel Strategies

### Channel 1: The Referral Loop (The Engine)

FinTech has proven that direct cash bounties are mathematically the cheapest way to acquire active users.
| The Mechanism | Why it Works | The Defense against Fraud |
|---------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| "Invite a friend. When they deposit £100, you both get £50." | Current users vouch for the app. The £50 bounty is cheaper than a £150 Facebook Ad. | **Critical limit**: We only pay out _after_ the invited user passes KYC and leaves the £100 deposited for 14 days. This prevents bot-farming. |

### Channel 2: The "Freelance Creator" Niche

We aren't targeting "Finance Tech" influencers; they are saturated. We are targeting "How to be a Graphic Designer" influencers.
| Action | Execution |
|----------|----------|
| YouTuber Target | "Ali Abdaal", "Chris Do", or mid-tier UI/UX designers. |
| The Pitch | Pay them £3,000 for a 60-second integration: _"If you are taking freelance design clients, do not mix it with your personal cash. Use SoloFi to auto-save for tax."_ |

### Channel 3: The "Waitlist Drop" (Hype Mechanics)

| Strategy              | Execution                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Manufactured Scarcity | The app is "Invite Only" for the first 6 months.                                                                                                             |
| The Golden Ticket     | You get 3 "Golden Tickets" to invite your freelance friends. We make the physical debit card glow-in-the-dark solely so it gets posted on Instagram Stories. |
