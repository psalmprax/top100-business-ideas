# Product Roadmap: LedgerBank

## Phase 1: "The Tax-Aware Ledger" (Week 1-4) [MVP]

- **Core Feature**: Business account with automatic VAT/Income Tax estimation per transaction.
- **Tax Pot 2.0**: Automated transfers into a protected "HMRC Reserve."
- **Dashboard**: "Real-time Profit vs. Taxable Income."

## Phase 2: "MTD Bridge" (Month 1-3)

- **HMRC Integration**: Direct API connection for Quarterly MTD updates.
- **Expense OCR**: Scan receipts and automatically link them to bank transactions for deduction verification.
- **VAT Engine**: Automatically handle cross-border VAT (Reversed Charge, etc.) for digital services.

## Phase 3: "Full Financial Ops" (Month 4-6)

- **Pension Auto-sweep**: Connect a SIPP and auto-contribute to lower tax liability.
- **Credit Card**: Business card that requires receipt capture for every purchase _before_ the transaction finalizes (Zero-loss bookkeeping).
- **Global Expansion**: Localized tax engines for Ireland, Germany, and Spain.
