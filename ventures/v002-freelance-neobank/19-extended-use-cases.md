# Extended Use Cases: LedgerBank (Freelance Neobank)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)

### Use Case 1: Real-Time Making Tax Digital (MTD) Projection
**The Competitor Way**: A freelancer gets a shock tax bill each year because they didn't save enough. They use a spreadsheet to estimate taxes, but it's always wrong.
**The LedgerBank Override**: LedgerBank connects to HMRC and displays a real-time "Tax Projection" widget. As you earn money, it automatically calculates your estimated tax liability based on your specific income profile. It suggests a "Savings Target" and auto-splits your income into a tax savings bucket.

### Use Case 2: Zero-Touch Tax Filing
**The Competitor Way**: A freelancer spends 8 hours gathering receipts, filling out forms, and uploading to HMRC.
**The LedgerBank Override**: LedgerBank automatically categorizes every transaction. At tax time, it pre-fills your Self Assessment return. You review it, click "File," and you're done. No manual data entry.

### Use Case 3: The "Payment on Account" Loan
**The Competitor Way**: A freelancer has a £10,000 tax bill due in January but won't have the cash until April.
**The LedgerBank Override**: LedgerBank offers an instant "Tax Loan" at 5% APR. It's integrated with your tax calculation, so the loan amount exactly matches your HMRC payment on account. The loan is repaid automatically from your future income.

### Use Case 11: Full Mobile Banking Hub (Native iOS/Android)
**Scenario**: A globe-trotting freelancer needs to block a lost card, exchange currency, and scan a receipt while waiting for a flight at an international airport.
**Solution**: LedgerBank offers a full-featured native mobile app. Unlike mobile web wrappers, it provides biometric security, instant push notifications for tax targets, and a high-performance scanning engine for instant receipt categorization, even on low-bandwidth connections.

### Use Case 12: Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) API for Developers
**Scenario**: A freelance developer building a custom project management tool wants to embed their real-time tax-savings balance and transaction history directly into their own dashboard.
**Solution**: LedgerBank provides a Public Banking API (BaaS). Developers can securely fetch their balances, categorize transactions via API, and even trigger internal transfers programmatically, turning their bank account into a programmable asset.

### Use Case 13: Offline-First Transaction Ledger
**Scenario**: A photographer working in a remote area without data coverage needs to log a cash expense and ensure their tax projection remains accurate.
**Solution**: The LedgerBank app features an "Offline Ledger." All manual entries and scans are stored locally with an encrypted time-stamp. As soon as the device reconnects, the ledger syncs with the central database, recalculating the MTD (Making Tax Digital) projection in seconds.

### Use Case 14: 24/7 "Wealth Concierge" Support SLA
**Scenario**: A high-earning freelancer's account is flagged for a large incoming international wire transfer on a Friday evening, potentially delaying their weekend plans.
**Solution**: Enterprise/Premium tier members have access to a 24/7 "Wealth Concierge." A dedicated human account manager is available around the clock to resolve compliance flags, expedite wire transfers, and provide immediate clarity on tax-saving strategies.
