## Extended Use Cases: EstatePlan (Digital Estate Planner)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)

### Use Case 1: Automated Digital Asset Discovery
**The Competitor Way**: Users must manually list every account (Twitter, Gmail, Crypto) themselves. If they forget one, it's lost.
**The EstatePlan Override**: AI-powered discovery tool that helps users identify their "Digital Footprint" by scanning receipts, emails, and social handles to ensure no high-value asset is left behind in the legacy plan.

### Use Case 2: Smart "Proof of Life" Triggers
**The Competitor Way**: Plans are triggered manually by a lawyer or family member, often months after the event.
**The EstatePlan Override**: Customizable "Activity Pulse" monitors specific digital footprints. If inactivity is detected across defined nodes for 30+ days, it initiates a secure "Proof of Life" multi-step verification before executing the digital legacy transfer.

### Use Case 3: Multi-Jurisdictional Digital Custody
**The Competitor Way**: Simple password managers that store everything in one cloud, vulnerable to court orders or single-point failure.
**The EstatePlan Override**: Cryptographic "Sharding" of access keys across geographically distributed vaults. This ensures that legal transfers comply with both local inheritance laws and global privacy standards.

## Extended Use Cases (4-13)

### Use Case 4: Guided Heir Selection & Setup (Self-Service)
**Scenario**: User knows they need a plan but is overwhelmed and doesn't know where to start.
**Solution**: 5-minute "Legacy Onboarding" wizard. AI asks simple questions about social handles, crypto, and family, and automatically generates a "Skeleton Plan" with prioritized setup tasks for the user to complete at their own pace.

### Use Case 5: Institutional Custody Integration (SLA)
**Scenario**: User wants the same security for their digital assets as their physical safe.
**Solution**: Tiered "Security Custody" SLA. Partners with regulated custodians (e.g., Coinbase Custody for crypto, secure banking partitions for documents) to provide enterprise-grade "Cold Storage" for sensitive estate information.

### Use Case 6: Multi-Jurisdictional Inheritance Mapping (Localization)
**Scenario**: User has assets in France, the UK, and the USA, each with different digital inheritance laws.
**Solution**: Localized legal cross-mapping. The system flags that "Digital Rights" in France differ from "Uniform Fiduciary Access" rules in the US and adjusts the instruction manuals for executors based on the asset's specific jurisdiction.

### Use Case 7: Social Media "Legacy Contact" Sync
**Scenario**: User wants their Instagram memorialized but their Facebook profile deleted.
**Solution**: Direct API integration with social platform "Legacy" settings. Automatically pushes the user's pre-defined wishes to the platforms' native death-notification APIs.

### Use Case 8: Subscription "Kill-Switch" for Executors
**Scenario**: Heirs don't realize the deceased is still paying $500/mo in enterprise SaaS subscriptions.
**Solution**: Integration with **CostControl (v102)** to provide executors with an "Active Payables" report and automated templates to notify banks to stop payments.

### Use Case 9: Family Photo "Vault" Curator
**Scenario**: 10,000 photos stored in Apple Photos/Google Photos; heirs only want the "Highlights."
**Solution**: AI-curated "Legacy Album." Scans the cloud drive and identifies the top 500 "High-Sentiment" photos based on metadata (faces, locations, favorites) to present to family first.

### Use Case 10: Encrypted Message from the Grave
**Scenario**: User wants to share a final video/text message with their children 1 year after passing.
**Solution**: "Time-Released Encrypted Capsules." Messages are encrypted with a key that is only re-constituted and shared with the recipient on a specific anniversary after the verified transition.

### Use Case 11: Real-time Estate "Safety" Score
**Scenario**: User buys a new laptop or opens a new crypto wallet and forgets to update the map.
**Solution**: "Safety Audit" crawler. The app pings the user if it detects new "High-Value" account registrations or if a linked vault hasn't been refreshed in 180 days.

### Use Case 12: Professional Executor Interface (SLA)
**Scenario**: A law firm is appointed as the professional executor.
**Solution**: Dedicated "Solicitor Portal" with legal-grade audit trails. Ensures that every action taken by the executor is logged for probate court verification, meeting 99.9% data availability SLAs.
