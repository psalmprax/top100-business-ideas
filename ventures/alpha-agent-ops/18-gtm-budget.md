# GTM Budget & Strategy: Agent Ops Sentinel

## 📊 GTM Strategy Overview

Because our end-user is a highly technical developer or AI Engineer, traditional B2B marketing (Facebook Ads, Cold Calling) will actively hurt our brand. We must rely on **Developer Relations (DevRel)**, Open Source credibility, and highly technical content marketing.

### Channel Mix (Year 1)

| Channel       | % of Budget | Monthly Spend | Core Activity                                                      |
| ------------- | ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Content / SEO | 40%         | £4,000        | Technical deep-dives, architectural teardowns.                     |
| Sponsorships  | 30%         | £3,000        | Sponsoring AI Hackathons, niche newsletters (TLDR AI).             |
| Events (Dev)  | 20%         | £2,000        | Sending engineers to conferences (Reactathon, AI Engineer Summit). |
| Paid Search   | 10%         | £1,000        | High intent bottom-of-funnel Google Ads ("stop openai loop").      |

## 🎯 Channel Strategies

### Content/SEO (The "Trojan Horse")

Engineers search for pain points, not products.
| Tactic | Target Keyword | Example Title |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Architecture Post | `langchain cost control` | "How we stopped Langchain from burning $1,000 in 2 hours." |
| Tutorial | `prevent bot loops` | "Building a semantic rate-limiter in Rust for GPT-4." |
| Open Source | `llm proxy` | "Why we open sourced our 5ms LLM Edge Proxy." |

### Hackathons & Community (Grassroots)

| Action                      | Investment             | Return                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sponsor AGI House Hackathon | £2,000 for pizza/prize | Get 50 top-tier AI engineers to integrate Sentinel over the weekend. |
| Discord Community           | Free (Time)            | Real-time bug reports and feature requests.                          |

### Mid-Market Sales Motion

Once a developer integrates Sentinel, we wait until their volume hits >5M tokens/month.
Our Founder / AE looks up the company on LinkedIn, finds the "VP of Engineering," and sends a "Value Trap" email:

> "Hi [VP], your engineer [Name] successfully deployed Sentinel last week. We blocked 40 bad LLM loops across your app. I'd love to show you the Enterprise control plane so Finance can set hard budget caps per agent."
