## 🎯 Scenario Planning

### Conservative (Worst Case)

- **Runway**: 14 months (Assuming £500k Pre-Seed)
- **Milestones**: MVP launch, 20 customers. Developer adoption is slow, enterprise sales cycles take 6 months.
- **Risk**: Agents become cheaper natively, reducing the "Cost Savings" pitch. Must pivot entirely to "Security/Compliance" pitch.

### Base Case

- **Runway**: Default Alive by month 12
- **Milestones**: 50 Customers, £42k MRR. Developer-led growth augmented by enterprise compliance needs.
- **Risk**: Competitors like LangChain build basic loop-detection natively.

### Aggressive (Best Case)

- **Milestones**: 100+ customers by end of Y1, £1M+ ARR.
- **Trigger**: A major public failure of an autonomous agent at a Fortune 500 company causes panic-buying of guardrail software.
