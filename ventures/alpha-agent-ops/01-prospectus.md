# Venture Prospectus: Agent Ops (Autonomous Control Plane)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The $12,000 Oops"

As enterprises transition from "Chatbots" to "Autonomous Agents" (Agentic AI), they are losing control over visibility and costs. An agent given a vague instruction can enter an infinite reasoning loop, spinning up thousands of tokens or cloud instances in minutes.

- **Stats**: 40% of Agentic AI projects are predicted to be canceled by 2027 due to "unclear costs."
- **Current State**: Most companies use basic "Token Limits" which break the agent's logic. They need "Reasoning Guardrails."

## 🟢 The Solution: Agentic Sentinel

A real-time observability and governance layer that sits between the Agent Orchestrator (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen) and the LLM.

- **Key Innovation**: "Semantic Cost Capping"—the system understands _intent_ and kills loops before they exhaust project budgets, without killing the underlying agent's state.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **SaaS Tier**: $499/mo for up to 5 concurrent production agents.
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing based on throughput + % of "Saved Waste" (Loss Prevention model).

## 🌍 Market Potential

- **TAM**: $47B by 2030 (Agentic AI Market).
- **Urgency**: Highest. Companies are hitting "Pause" on AI agents _today_ out of fear.
