# Outreach Strategy: Pilot Testing 'Agent Ops'

## 🎯 Target Personas

- **CTO / Head of AI**: Worried about the "Shadow AI" costs popping up in the budget.
- **Cloud Architects**: Tasked with scaling AI agents without bankrupting the account.

## 🏢 Target Companies (Pilot Phase)

- **Mid-market SaaS**: (e.g., Intercom, Gong, HubSpot users) who are deploying agents for support.
- **AI Design Agencies**: High volume of agent testing, high waste.

## 💬 Outreach Scripts

### LinkedIn (Soft Approach)

> "Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] is pushing the boundaries on agentic workflows lately. We've been looking at the 'Agent Sprawl' problem—where agents get stuck in infinite reasoning loops that burn thousands in tokens overnight. I've built a 'Sentinel' proxy that catches these loops before they drain the budget. Would you be open to a 10-min chat on how we're capping costs without breaking agent logic?"

### The "Loop Audit" Offer (Hard Approach)

> "We've analyzed enterprise agent deployments and found an average of 15% 'Reasoning Waste'. We'll run a free audit on your last 1,000 agent sessions to show you exactly where the infinite loops are happening. Interested?"

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy

- **r/MLOps**: Post the "How I saved $5,000 in loops" technical breakdown.
- **Product Hunt**: Launch as a "Free for Devs" cost-tracker to build the top of the funnel.
