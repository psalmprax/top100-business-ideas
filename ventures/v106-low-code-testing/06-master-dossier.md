# Master Dossier: NoCodeQA

## Executive Summary
NoCodeQA is the first dedicated testing platform for no-code/low-code workflows. It enables citizen developers to create reliable automations without writing code.

## The Opportunity
- **TAM**: $3.2B by 2028.
- **Gap**: No testing tools for no-code.
- **Demand**: 58% have undetected errors.

## Business Model
- **Free**: 5 workflows.
- **Pro** ($49/mo): Unlimited.
- **Enterprise**: Custom.

## GTM
1. No-code community outreach.
2. Partnership with platforms.
3. Template marketplace.

## Risk Assessment
- **Medium**: New category.
- **Low**: Small investment required.
