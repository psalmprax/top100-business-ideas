# Product Roadmap: TaskFlow AI

## Phase 1: "Task Extraction" (Week 1-4) [MVP]
- **Core Feature**: Extract tasks from meeting audio.
- **Task Format**: Owner + Action + Deadline.
- **Integrations**: Export to Asana, Todoist.

## Phase 2: "Intelligence" (Month 2-3)
- **Consensus Mapping**: Track agreements vs. concerns.
- **Dependency Tracking**: Link tasks across meetings.
- **Follow-up Detection**: Remind about incomplete items.

## Phase 3: "Enterprise" (Month 4-6)
- **Analytics**: Team productivity insights.
- **CRM Integration**: Link tasks to deals.
- **Compliance**: PII redacted transcripts.
