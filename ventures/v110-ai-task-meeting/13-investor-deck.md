## 📝 Talking Points (Per Slide)

### Slide 2 - Problem

- Lead with a story
- Use specific numbers
- Make it relatable

### Slide 3 - Solution

- Show, don't just tell
- Focus on the "aha" moment
- Explain what makes it possible now

### Slide 6 - Business Model

- Be ready for "why this pricing?"
- Have unit economics memorized
- Know your gross margin

### Slide 7 - Traction

- Any traction is good traction
- Pre-revenue? Show interest/signups
- Use charts to show momentum

### Slide 12 - The Ask

- Have a clear use of funds
- Know your valuation range
- Be ready to negotiate
