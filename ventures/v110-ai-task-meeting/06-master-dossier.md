# Master Dossier: TaskFlow AI

## Executive Summary

TaskFlow AI extracts actionable tasks from meeting transcripts. Unlike Otter.ai or Fireflies which provide transcripts, TaskFlow identifies specific commitments, owners, and deadlines.

## The Opportunity

- **TAM**: $3.6B by 2028.
- **Gap**: 65% of action items never completed.
- **Need**: Accountability in meetings.

## Business Model

- **Free**: 3 meetings/month.
- **Pro** ($15/mo): Unlimited.
- **Team** ($29/user): Shared workspace.

## GTM

1. PM tool partnerships.
2. Sales team outreach.
3. Productivity content.

## Risk Assessment

- **Low**: Growing meeting intelligence market.
- **Medium**: Competition from well-funded players.
