# Venture Prospectus: TaskFlow AI (AI Meeting Task Generator)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Lost Action Item"
Meetings generate action items that get lost in transcripts. You spend hours reviewing notes to find what you promised to do.
- **Stats**: 65% of action items from meetings are never completed.
- **Current State**: Otter.ai and Fireflies provide transcripts but no actionable task extraction.

## 🟢 The Solution: TaskFlow AI
AI that extracts specific tasks from meeting transcripts with owners and deadlines.
- **Key Innovation**: "Commitment Detection"—identifies exact promises made in calls.

## 💰 Revenue Model
- **Free**: 3 meetings/month.
- **Pro** ($15/mo): Unlimited + integrations.
- **Team** ($29/user): Shared workspace.

## 🌍 Market Potential
- **TAM**: $3.6B by 2028 (Meeting Intelligence Market).
