# Outreach Strategy: ComplianceBot

## 🎯 Target Personas

- **Security Engineers**: Responsible for AI application security.
- **ML Engineers**: Building production AI systems.
- **CTOs**: Worried about AI liability and compliance.

## 🏢 Target Channels (Pilot Phase)

- **r/ArtificialIntelligence**, r/MLOps communities.
- **Hacker News**: Technical audience interested in AI security.
- **GitHub**: Open source maintainers.

## 💬 Outreach Scripts

### The "Headline" Hook

> "A developer accidentally pushed API keys and PII to GitHub. Traditional scanners missed it. ComplianceBot caught it in 3 seconds."

### The "Compliance" Approach

> "SOC2 auditors are now asking about AI security. We'll scan your repo and give you a compliance report in 10 minutes."

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy

- **Open Source**: Free for open source projects.
- **Tech blogs**: "How we found 50 AI vulnerabilities in popular libraries."
- **Conference talks**: AI security workshops.
