# Product Roadmap: ObligationTrack

## Phase 1: "Contract Upload" (Week 1-4) [MVP]

- **Core Feature**: PDF upload and basic metadata extraction.
- **Dashboard**: Calendar view of key dates.
- **Alerts**: Email reminders for renewals.

## Phase 2: "AI Extraction" (Month 2-3)

- **Smart Parsing**: Extract dates, values, parties automatically.
- **Clause Detection**: Identify termination, liability clauses.
- **Search**: Full-text search across contracts.

## Phase 3: "Enterprise" (Month 4-6)

- **M&A Support**: Due diligence filters.
- **API Access**: Integrate with legal systems.
- **E-Signature**: DocuSign integration.
