# Master Dossier: ObligationTrack

## Executive Summary
ObligationTrack is an AI-powered contract lifecycle management tool for mid-market companies. It automatically extracts key dates and clauses from contracts, alerting teams before renewals and terminations.

## The Opportunity
- **TAM**: $4.3B by 2028.
- **Gap**: Enterprise tools cost $50K+/year.
- **Demand**: 73% of companies miss renewals.

## Business Model
- **Starter** ($49/mo): 50 contracts.
- **Pro** ($149/mo): Unlimited.
- **Enterprise**: Custom.

## GTM
1. Free contract audits.
2. Legal/procurement outreach.
3. Partner with SaaS vendors.

## Risk Assessment
- **Low**: Proven market.
- **Medium**: Competition from well-funded startups.
