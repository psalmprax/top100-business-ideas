# Product Roadmap: SustainBase

## Phase 1: "Double Materiality Wizard" (Week 1-4) [MVP]

- **Core Feature**: Automated materiality assessment (Impact vs. Financial).
- **Stakeholder Survey**: Tool to collect data from employees/suppliers.
- **Gap Analysis**: Compare current data vs. CSRD mandatory disclosure requirements.

## Phase 2: "The Supply Chain Engine" (Month 2-4)

- **Scope 3 Capture**: Automated email/portal for suppliers to upload their carbon footprints.
- **ESRS Templates**: Pre-built JSON/PDF templates for the 12 topical standards.
- **Auditor Portal**: A "view-only" link with a cryptographic "Proof of Source" for every data point.

## Phase 3: "Real-Time ESG Ops" (Month 5-6)

- **IoT Integration**: Direct feed from smart meters and logistic fleet trackers.
- **Scenario Modeling**: "What if we switch to solar?" (Impact on upcoming report).
- **Taxonomy Matching**: Automated classification of activities against the EU Taxonomy.
