## 📋 Interview Guide Template

### Intro (2 min)
> "Hi [NAME], I'm building [VENTURE NAME], and I'd love to learn about your experience with [PROBLEM AREA]. This will take about 20 minutes. Everything you share is confidential."

### Questions

**Current State**
- Can you describe your current process for [ACTIVITY]?
- What tools do you use today?
- How much time do you spend on this weekly?

**Pain Points**
- What's the most frustrating part of [ACTIVITY]?
- How much does this cost you (time/money)?
- What have you tried to fix this?

**Solutions**
- Have you looked for solutions? What did you find?
- What would your ideal solution look like?
- How much would you pay for a solution?

**Closing**
- Would you be interested in testing a solution?
- Who else should I talk to?
- Can I follow up in 2 weeks?
