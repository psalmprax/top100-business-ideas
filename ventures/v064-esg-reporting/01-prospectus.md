# Venture Prospectus: SustainBase (Mid-Market ESG Compliance)

## 🔴 The Problem: "Timeline Compression"
The EU **Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)** is hitting the mid-market (250–2,000 employees) with force. 
- **The Pressure**: While large firms have SAP/Big 4 support, mid-market firms are being "squeezed" by their clients to provide auditable ESG data now.
- **The Gap**: Existing tools are either enterprise-level (thousands of dollars per month) or simple "Carbon Calculators" that don't meet the CSRD's **Double Materiality** requirements.

## 🟢 The Solution: SustainBase
A "Workstream-First" CSRD platform. It doesn't just calculate carbon; it manages the entire **Double Materiality Assessment** and **Gap Analysis** required for the 1,100+ data points in the ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards).
- **Key Innovation**: "Evidence Ledger"—A system that tags every ESG claim to a specific invoice, electricity bill, or policy doc, making it "Audit-Ready" for third-party assurance.

## 💰 Revenue Model
- **Platform Fee**: €3,000/year base SaaS.
- **Reporting Module**: €1,500 per annual CSRD Report export.
- **Auditor Access**: Free for accounting firms to login and verify client data.

## 🌍 Market Potential
- **TAM**: 50,000+ EU mid-market companies now in-scope for CSRD.
- **Urgency**: First reporting cycle starts for many in 2025/2026.
