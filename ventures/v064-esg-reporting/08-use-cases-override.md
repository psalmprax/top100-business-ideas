# Competitive Override Use Cases: SustainBase (CSRD)

Current software focuses mostly on "Carbon Accounting" (Scope 1, 2, 3) for enterprise sustainability reports. The new EU CSRD mandates massive **Double Materiality** reporting across 1,100 data points (social, governance, biodiversity), not just carbon. **SustainBase** is built specifically for the Mid-Market to handle the full breadth of the ESRS standards.

Here are the specific use cases where SustainBase surpasses the competition:

## Use Case 1: The Cryptographic "Evidence Ledger" (For Auditors)
**The Competitor Way**: A company generates a beautiful PDF report claiming they reduced water usage by 10%. When the Big-4 Auditor requests proof, the company scrambles through Google Drive to find a 14-month-old water bill.
**The Sentinel Override**: Every data point entered into SustainBase requires an attached "Proof Document" (e.g., a PDF invoice, a signed HR policy). SustainBase creates a "Read-Only Auditor Portal." When an auditor clicks the "10% Water Reduction" metric, the exact water bill PDF opens instantly, cryptographically hashed to prove it hasn't been altered since upload.

## Use Case 2: Supply-Chain "Scope 3" Portal for SMEs
**The Competitor Way**: A mid-market manufacturer sends a complex, 40-page Excel spreadsheet to a small supplier (a 5-person machine shop) asking them to calculate their carbon footprint. The supplier ignores it.
**The Sentinel Override**: SustainBase provides a heavily stripped-down "One-Click Supplier Portal." The supplier receives a magic link. They don't need to know carbon math; the portal simply asks: *"Upload your last 3 electricity bills and tell us how many delivery trucks you own."* SustainBase's OCR engine parses the bills and calculates the Scope 3 emissions automatically for the parent company.

## Use Case 3: Automated ESRS Gap Analysis mapping
**The Competitor Way**: Paying a consultant €40k to read the 450 pages of the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) and figure out which of the 1,100 data points actually apply to a mid-market shoe retailer.
**The Sentinel Override**: The "Double Materiality Wizard." A company answers a 20-minute onboarding questionnaire about their business model, supply chain, and revenue streams. SustainBase's AI maps these answers against the ESRS and instantly highlights the exact 150 data points (out of 1,100) that are legally mandated for *their specific business*, instantly defining the scope of the project.
