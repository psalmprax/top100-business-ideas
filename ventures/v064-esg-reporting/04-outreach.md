# Outreach Strategy: SustainBase (CSRD Capture)

## 🎯 Target Personas
- **Sustainability Manager**: Tasked with a report they don't have tools for.
- **CFO**: Worried about the financial materiality aspect of CSRD.
- **External Auditor**: Looking for a way to verify their client's ESG data without 1,000 emails.

## 🏢 Target Channels (Pilot Phase)
- **Mid-Tier Audit Firms (non-Big 4)**: (e.g., BDO, Grant Thornton, Mazars) who need a tool for their clients.
- **Manufacturing Supply Chains**: German and Italian mid-market industrial firms.
- **CSRD Training Workshops**: Sponsor local chambers of commerce seminars.

## 💬 Outreach Scripts
### The "Supply Chain Pressure" Approach
> "Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] is a key supplier to [Large Client]. With CSRD going live, [Large Client] is likely going to demand auditable ESG data from you this year. We've built SustainBase to help mid-market firms handle Double Materiality and Supply Chain reporting without needing a Big 4 budget. Can I send you our 'CSRD Readiness Checklist' for suppliers?"

### The "Audit Efficiency" Approach (to Auditing Firms)
> "Hi [Name], your clients are likely sending you messy spreadsheets for their ESG audits. We've built an 'Evidence Ledger' that links every ESG data point directly to a policy or bill, making your job 10x faster. We're looking for 3 mid-tier firms to beta-test our Auditor Portal. Interested?"

## 🔗 Viral Channel Strategy
- **LinkedIn**: "CSRD for Mid-Market" educational series.
- **Webinars**: "Double Materiality for Dummies"—solving the most misunderstood part of the directive.
