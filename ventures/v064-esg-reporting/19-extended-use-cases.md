# Extended Use Cases: SustainBase (ESG Reporting)

## Core Use Cases (1-3)

### Use Case 1: The Evidence Ledger

**The Competitor Way**: An ESG manager manually collects PDFs of utility bills, sustainability certifications, and audit reports, then tries to organize them into a coherent data room for auditors.
**The SustainBase Override**: SustainBase automatically collects evidence from 50+ data sources (utility APIs, HR systems, supply chain databases). It maps every data point to the specific ESG framework requirement (GHG Protocol, TCFD, SASB). When an auditor asks for "Scope 2 emissions evidence for Q3 2024," you click one button and the evidence package is generated.

### Use Case 2: The Supply Chain Portal

**The Competitor Way**: You send Excel templates to 500 suppliers asking for their carbon emissions. 47 respond. You spend 3 months chasing the rest.
**The SustainBase Override**: SustainBase gives every supplier a "Supplier Portal." Suppliers upload their emissions data once. It automatically cascades up to your corporate dashboard. Suppliers get a sustainability score they can use to win business from other buyers.

### Use Case 3: The ESRS Gap Analysis

**The Competitor Way**: You hire a consultant to read the 1,200-page ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards) and manually map your current data to it. The deliverable is a 50-page PDF that is obsolete 3 weeks later.
**The SustainBase Override**: SustainBase continuously scans your data against the latest ESRS requirements. It highlights gaps in real-time: "You have Scope 3 data for Tier 1 suppliers but not Tier 2." It gives you a prioritized roadmap to close each gap.

### Use Case 11: Mobile Field Evidence Collector (Audit Prep)

**Scenario**: An environmental safety officer at a remote manufacturing site needs to capture evidence of water treatment compliance while walking the floor, away from their desk.
**Solution**: SustainBase provides a specialized mobile collector app. It allows users to take geo-tagged, time-stamped photos of physical evidence (meters, filters, safety logs) and voice-to-text audit notes. This data is instantly hashed and uploaded to the Evidence Ledger (UC1), making it immutable and audit-ready before the officer even returns to their office.

### Use Case 12: 24/7 Regulatory Response Desk (SLA)

**Scenario**: A multinational corporation receives an urgent "Request for Information" (RFI) from an EU regulator on a Friday night, requiring detailed Scope 3 emission data by Monday morning.
**Solution**: Enterprise-tier SustainBase includes a 24/7 "Regulatory Response" SLA. Our senior ESG analysts are available around the clock to help clients navigate complex RFIs, verify evidence gaps, and ensure that the generated report meets the highest standard of assurance for regulatory scrutiny.
