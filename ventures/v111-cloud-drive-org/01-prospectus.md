# Venture Prospectus: CloudSync (Cross-Platform Drive Org)

## 🔴 The Problem: "The Scattered Files"

Your files are spread across Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive. You can never find what you need when you need it.

- **Stats**: Workers waste 1.5 hours/day searching for files.
- **Current State**: No tool aggregates and searches across all clouds.

## 🟢 The Solution: CloudSync

Unified search and organization across all cloud drives.

- **Key Innovation**: "Cross-Cloud Intelligence"—search once, find everywhere.

## 💰 Revenue Model

- **Free**: 1,000 files indexed.
- **Pro** ($9/mo): Unlimited + deduplication.
- **Team** ($24/user): Shared workspaces.

## 🌍 Market Potential

- **TAM**: $2.8B by 2028 (File Management Market).
